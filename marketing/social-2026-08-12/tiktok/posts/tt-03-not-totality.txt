Britain does not go dark on Wednesday. Sorry.

The totality track runs through Iceland and northern Spain. From here it's a deep partial — 88 to 96% of the Sun covered, deepest in the far south-west and shallower the further north you go. Peak 19:12 in London, 19:05 in Edinburgh, 19:16 in Truro.

Nine-tenths gone is not darkness. It is the light going strange: flat, silver, shadows too sharp, birds confused. It is genuinely worth going outside for. It is just not night.

Still the deepest eclipse over Britain since August 1999.

Never look at the Sun directly. ISO 12312-2 glasses, a pinhole projection onto card, or simply watch the light change.
