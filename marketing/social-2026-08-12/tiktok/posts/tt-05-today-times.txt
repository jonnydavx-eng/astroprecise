The Sun does not set eclipsed here, whatever else you read today. It clears the Moon around 20:10 and sets after that — roughly 20:22 in Dover, 20:29 in London, 20:46 in Penzance. All of it low in the west, so a hill or a rooftop will simply take it from you. Pick your horizon before six.

Greatest eclipse globally is 17:46 UT, over the Arctic. Totality crosses Greenland, Iceland and northern Spain — not Britain. From here it's a deep partial, and it's the deepest since 1999.

Then the Perseids peak the same night. The sky is showing off.

ISO 12312-2 glasses or a pinhole projection. Never bare eyes, even at 91%.
