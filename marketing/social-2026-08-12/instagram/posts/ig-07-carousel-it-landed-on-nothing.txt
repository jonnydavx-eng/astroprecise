For a lot of you, yesterday's eclipse did nothing to your chart. We would like to say that out loud, the morning after, when saying it costs us something.

Here is the honest mechanism. An eclipse is a point — yesterday's was 20°02′ Leo. Whether the astrological tradition has anything to say about it for you depends on whether that point lands close to something in your own chart. Most of a chart is empty space. So for most people, most eclipses: nothing within orb. A quiet one.

Our free page has always been built to say so. If the eclipse makes no close contact with your chart, the page says so plainly, before it recommends anything to you. We wrote that gate in before there was anything to sell, and it stays in when there is — it will cost us the easiest sale on the site. We would rather have the reputation.

The sky is not a message service. It is a set of moving bodies whose positions can be computed to the arcminute, centuries in either direction. That is quite enough romance for us.
