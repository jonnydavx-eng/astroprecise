It's today.

Times for Britain, all BST, all low in the west:
First contact ≈18:00 — the first bite out of the Sun's edge.
Maximum ≈19:05 — about 91% covered in the south, a little less further north.
Last contact ≈20:10.

All of it happens low over the western horizon, so a hill, a rooftop or a line of trees will simply take it from you. Choose your spot before six.

Safety, once: ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses, or pinhole projection onto card, or just watch the light change around you. Never bare eyes. Britain never reaches totality, so there is no moment this evening when it is safe to look directly.

Then don't go inside. The Perseids peak tonight, and because a solar eclipse can only happen at new moon, there is no moonlight to wash them out. The best conditions in years, handed to us on the same night.
