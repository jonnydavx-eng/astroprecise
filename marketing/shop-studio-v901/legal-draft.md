## 1. Owner facts required before publication

The draft cannot become public contract terms until the owner supplies and approves:

- legal name and any trading name;
- legal form, for example sole trader or company;
- **full geographic business/service address** suitable for public pre-contract disclosure;
- direct electronic contact route for service/cancellation notices;
- VAT registration and tax position;
- territories in which orders will be accepted;
- governing-law/jurisdiction wording appropriate to the operator;
- confirmation of the five-working-day production target;
- confirmation of Whole Sky's one reasonable layout adjustment requested within seven calendar days;
- confirmation of the 30-day local raw-input/working-file deletion rule;
- the applicable accounting-record retention schedule;
- Gumroad account age, Commission eligibility, payout identity and seller-of-record/merchant-of-record treatment for these services.
- approval of the gift-recipient legitimate-interests assessment and its adult-only, recipient-self-entry safeguards;
- confirmation that gift files go to the recipient by default and that buyer-copy disclosure requires the recipient's separate unticked authorisation.
- provider-specific controller/processor roles, data fields, sub-processors, locations, international-transfer mechanisms and retention;
- an owner-approved data-protection complaint owner, monitored electronic route and case-record process.

The public address is a hard blocker. A contact form, social profile, PO box without a geographic establishment address, or Gumroad profile alone should not be treated as a substitute without professional confirmation.

## 2. Proposed contract classification

Treat each order conservatively as a **commissioned personalised service ending in digital-content delivery**, not as an instant download and not as a physical good.

This matters because:

- the buyer requests personalised calculation, composition, rendering and quality assurance after the order;
- the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 service-cancellation rules should be honoured;
- the “made to the consumer's specifications” exception for goods should not be used to erase a service cancellation right;
- the digital-content immediate-supply exception should not be used as a shortcut around the commissioned service rules;
- the Consumer Rights Act 2015 service and digital-content standards remain relevant;
- the £42 threshold associated with certain off-premises contracts should not be treated as an internet distance-selling exemption.

## 4. Proposed service terms

### 4.1 Scope and products

AstroPrecise offers only:

- Natal Sky Print Pack — £18;
- Personal Sky Keepsake — £29;
- Whole Sky Edition — £39.

Each is a personalised Gumroad Commission service ending in digital files. Each has two purchase modes—`self` and `gift`—at the same listed price; gift mode is not a fourth SKU. Nothing physical is included. Ko-fi contributions are voluntary support and do not form a Studio order, reserve a slot, change delivery timing or unlock a product.

### 4.2 Self and gift inputs

For `self`, the buyer must supply their own exact recorded birth date, local clock time, city and country, plus the display name requested for the files. Unknown, approximate or rectified times are not accepted. The service does not include birth-time rectification.

For `gift`, distinguish the **buyer** (the adult who pays and holds the consumer contract) from the **recipient** (the adult whose chart and files are created). The recipient pays nothing. The recipient must be present in the private checkout/intake, enter their own birth details, receive just-in-time privacy information and affirm that they are the data subject and want AstroPrecise to prepare the commission. The buyer must not enter, upload, forward or dictate those details.

Record a separate hash-bound buyer attestation that the buyer did not enter or direct the recipient's data. It is an accountability safeguard, not a substitute for recipient self-entry evidence.

Do not offer surprise gifts, delayed voucher redemption, gifts for under-18s or buyer-entered third-party birth data at launch. Do not infer recipient agreement from the buyer's payment, general terms acceptance, relationship to the recipient or possession of the recipient's details.

Gift mode may collect only an optional from-name and short birthday message in addition to necessary recipient-controlled fields. Warn beside that free-text field that it must not contain birth details, health information, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual-life information or other special-category data; it is not an open-ended reading brief. Gift mode adds a standalone A4 dedication jacket, phone reveal and square computed Moon-phase plate. The Moon artwork must state that it is calculated for the exact recorded birth UTC instant and is not a photograph. The reading PDF remains exactly 20 pages.

The buyer must check submitted details. AstroPrecise should validate format, calendar validity, place ambiguity, timezone and daylight-saving ambiguity before production. If inputs are incomplete or ambiguous, pause and request clarification privately.

### 4.3 Contract and payment

An order is a request for a commission. The owner must define exactly when AstroPrecise accepts it; a request or deposit must not silently become acceptance. State the approved formation event in the final public terms and durable confirmation. Do not begin work until the exact durable confirmation and required gift-recipient notice have been sent and preserved. If the service cannot be accepted, reject it promptly and return the deposit through the tested payment route.

The final checkout must accurately state the electronic-contract steps proved in the signed-in platform test: select one of the three SKUs and `self`/`gift` mode; enter and review the required fields; correct errors before submission; accept the governing terms; make any separate optional choices; submit the order request; and receive the operator's acceptance or rejection. State whether and how the contract is filed and accessible, the technical means for correcting input errors, and that the contract language is English. The durable acceptance attachment is the customer's retained contract record. Do not copy these steps into live terms until the real checkout has been tested against them.

The total service price is £18, £29 or £39. Planning copy assumes a Gumroad Commission flow with a 50% up-front deposit and remaining 50% after completion, based on current public help. Treat that as **draft and unverified for this seller account**, not a contractual fact. The exact account-visible deposit, balance, completion trigger, final-charge timing, tax/platform role and file-replacement route must be proved in an unpublished signed-in seller test and then copied exactly into the live pre-contract information.

No unwatermarked final file may be created or released merely because an order ID, 50% deposit or locally authored payment note exists. The local fulfilment gate remains closed until a seller-authenticated adapter verifies the matching paid-in-full state.

### 4.4 Production time

Subject to owner confirmation, the target is five working days beginning only when:

- complete, valid inputs are held;
- the order has been accepted; and
- either the buyer has expressly requested early performance or the 14-day cancellation period has ended.

“Working day” should be defined as Monday to Friday excluding bank holidays in England and Wales. If a clarification is needed, pause the clock and tell the buyer what is required.

### 4.5 Delivery and formats

Delivery is through the private Gumroad Commission/order route. Self-order files go to the buyer. Gift-order files go to the recipient by default. Files use generic names and include a customer SHA-256 manifest.

Sending recipient files to the buyer requires a separate, specific, informed and initially unticked recipient authorisation after the recipient sees what will be shared. It is not bundled with gift confirmation, early start or general terms. Refusal must not block the order or change the recipient's files. Record the exact notice version, SHA-256 wording hash, affirmative state and timestamp. The recipient may withdraw at any time: withdrawal before first dispatch prevents that copy, and later withdrawal prevents further buyer copies or replacements. Do not promise that a disclosure already completed can be recalled.

The A3 plate is an RGB home-print file. It is not represented as a commercial CMYK press master and does not claim bleed, TrimBox or OutputIntent. The Whole Sky Observatory still is visibly labelled `SCHEMATIC`; it uses computed body positions in an authored compressed view and is not a photograph, live feed or true-scale scientific image.

### 4.6 Corrections and layout adjustment

AstroPrecise corrects its calculation, transcription, rendering, missing-file and production errors without charge. This does not restrict statutory repair, repeat-performance, price-reduction, refund or other rights.

Whole Sky also includes one reasonable layout adjustment requested within seven calendar days after delivery. Define “reasonable” before launch as a visual/layout change using the same accepted birth inputs and agreed deliverables, not a new chart, changed birth record, new written commission or commercial licence.

## 5. Cancellation and early performance

### 5.1 Standard cancellation route

For a distance service contract, give the consumer 14 days after the day the contract is entered into to cancel without giving a reason, unless the right has lawfully ended after full performance.

The buyer may cancel using the model form below or any other clear statement through the private Gumroad order conversation and the published direct contact route. Record when the cancellation was sent. In gift mode, the recipient may separately refuse or stop processing of their data; that is a data-protection request, not a waiver of the buyer's consumer rights.

Personalisation alone does not justify saying “no cancellations” or “all sales final.”

### 5.2 Optional early-start request

If the buyer does not request early performance, do not begin commissioned work until the 14-day period has ended.

Use a separate, optional and initially unticked control:

> I expressly request AstroPrecise to begin this personalised service during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that if I cancel after work has begun, I may have to pay an amount proportionate to the service supplied up to cancellation; and that if the service is fully performed within that period, I will lose my right to cancel once it is complete.

This choice must not be bundled with general terms acceptance or required to place an order. Preserve the exact notice version, SHA-256 wording hash, affirmative state and timestamp in the durable contract record. An unselected control has no consent timestamp and cannot authorise work.

If completed digital files will be supplied during the cancellation period, obtain a second, separate, optional and initially unticked express consent and acknowledgement for early digital supply. Do not infer it from the service early-start request, general terms or buyer-copy authorisation. Use this exact wording:

> I expressly consent to AstroPrecise supplying my commissioned digital files during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that I lose the cancellation right for that digital content when supply begins. This does not affect my rights if the service or files are faulty, misdescribed or not supplied with reasonable care and skill.

Preserve its exact notice version, SHA-256 wording hash, affirmative state and timestamp. An unselected control has no consent timestamp and prohibits digital supply during the cancellation period. Reproduce both early-start and early-supply wording and choices in the durable confirmation.

If the consumer requested early performance and then cancels before completion, any charge must be proportionate to the service actually supplied up to cancellation, based on the total contract price. Do not charge for work in the cancellation period if the required information/request was not properly obtained.

The cancellation right ends on full performance during the 14-day period only where the required express request and acknowledgement were obtained. Confirm completion and the consequence on a durable medium.

### 5.3 Model cancellation form

The final public form must include the completed trader name, geographic address and electronic contact route.

A consumer may use any other clear cancellation statement; the model form is not mandatory.

## 6. Refunds, defects and complaints

Keep these routes separate:

- **Cancellation:** statutory distance-contract route described above.
- **Rejected commission:** refund the deposit promptly through Gumroad.
- **AstroPrecise error/defect:** correct or reperform within a reasonable time and without significant inconvenience; provide any further statutory remedy where correction/reperformance is impossible or not timely.
- **Buyer-requested scope change:** quote separately only after a clear new agreement; do not relabel a legal correction as a paid change.
- **Whole Sky layout adjustment:** one reasonable request within seven calendar days, as defined above.

Do not make Gumroad's platform policy the ceiling on statutory rights. Do not promise a blanket satisfaction guarantee that the operator cannot administer.

For a cancellation refund, reimburse payments received, less only any lawful proportionate amount due for service expressly requested and supplied during the cancellation period. Reimbursement must be made without undue delay and no later than 14 days after AstroPrecise is informed of the cancellation, using the same means of payment as the original transaction unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise, with no reimbursement fee. A gift refund goes to the buyer who paid. If the tested provider route cannot support those mechanics, checkout remains closed.

## 7. Intellectual property and permitted use

For a self order, the buyer is the licensee and receives a personal, non-commercial licence to download, keep backup copies, display privately and self-print the supplied files. For a gift, the adult recipient is the licensee and receives those same rights. The buyer has no right to receive, keep or use recipient files unless the recipient separately authorises a buyer copy and that authorisation remains valid at the relevant dispatch.

If a buyer copy is authorised, the buyer may keep one private copy and privately transfer or print it for that recipient as part of the gift. This does not grant an independent licence to analyse, publish, exploit or share the recipient's chart or reading. Neither party may resell, sublicense, publish for public download, redistribute, mint as a token or use the files in advertising without separate written permission.

This licence does not restrict statutory consumer rights. AstroPrecise retains its copyright and brand rights; third-party font/software material remains under its own licence.

## 8. Interpretive-content boundary

Chart positions and page metadata are calculated from the accepted birth inputs. Astrological passages are authored traditional symbolic interpretations for reflection and entertainment.

Do not describe the reading as:

- a scientific personality or psychological assessment;
- a diagnosis, treatment, prediction or certainty;
- medical, mental-health, legal, financial or safeguarding advice;
- a guaranteed outcome, compatibility verdict or decision-making substitute.

These limitations do not excuse inaccurate calculation, missing files, careless service or a product that is not as described.

## 9. Privacy and retention draft

### 9.1 Data and purpose

Process only what is needed to accept, calculate, render, quality-check, deliver, support, correct and account for the commission:

- buyer identity and contact/order details;
- for gift mode, adult recipient delivery contact, recipient confirmation, optional from-name/message and any separate buyer-copy authorisation;
- exact birth date, local time, city/country and derived coordinates/timezone;
- display name, product options and consent records;
- payment/order evidence and support history;
- generated files, quality manifests and correction history.

Use contract necessity as the proposed lawful basis for buyer/self-order processing and legal obligation for required accounting records, subject to owner/adviser confirmation. Contract necessity does not automatically cover recipient data where the contract is with the buyer.

For gift-recipient data, the proposed basis is legitimate interests: preparing and privately delivering the birthday commission the adult recipient has personally requested. Complete and approve `docs/GIFT-PRIVACY-LIA-DRAFT.md` before processing. The purpose, necessity and balancing tests must be documented; if a less intrusive flow can achieve the purpose or the recipient's rights/expectations override the interest, do not process. Recipient self-entry, just-in-time notice, easy objection/stopping, adult-only scope, no surprise route, recipient-default delivery, short retention and access restriction are mandatory safeguards.

Treat optional disclosure of recipient files to the buyer as consent-based: separate, specific, informed, freely given and initially unticked, with withdrawal available at any time. Withdrawal stops every buyer copy or replacement not already sent. Do not reuse birth inputs for marketing, profiling, training, public examples or unrelated research. Do not request health, belief, sexual-life or other special-category information.

Explicitly tell the recipient, separately from other information, that they may object at any time to legitimate-interests processing for reasons relating to their situation. Pause future work while assessing an objection and stop unless the controller can document compelling legitimate grounds that override the recipient's rights or the processing is needed for legal claims. Buyer-copy consent must be as easy to withdraw as to give and may be withdrawn at any time; withdrawal stops every copy or replacement that has not already been sent without affecting recipient delivery. Do not promise that a file already sent can be technically recalled.

### 9.2 Storage and access

- Keep real order inputs and authenticated payment receipts outside the repository in private per-order storage.
- Do not put birth data in a URL, command line, public form, analytics event, filename, log, sample or source control.
- Restrict access to the operator who needs it for fulfilment.
- Use the private Gumroad order conversation for clarifications.
- Do not email unencrypted raw birth/order JSON.
- Do not send recipient files or birth details to the buyer without the separately recorded buyer-copy authorisation.

### 9.3 Retention

Proposed rule, requiring owner confirmation:

- delete local raw birth inputs and local working files within 30 days after final delivery or the final agreed correction, whichever is later;
- keep only the minimum order/accounting evidence required by applicable law, separate from birth working files;
- document Gumroad's retention and deletion routes in the public privacy notice;
- test deletion as part of the end-to-end order.

HMRC's current self-employed guidance requires business records for at least five years after the 31 January submission deadline for the relevant tax year. The owner must confirm whether that rule or another company/VAT schedule applies; it is not a reason to retain raw birth working files unnecessarily.

### 9.4 Platforms and transfers

Gumroad handles order/payment information under its own terms and privacy arrangements. Confirm and accurately describe the controller/processor roles, data fields, sub-processors, destinations, provider-specific international-transfer mechanism, retention, buyer/recipient rights and contact route before launch. A generic claim that a data bridge, IDTA or standard clauses apply is not proof for a particular transfer. Do not describe Gumroad as eliminating AstroPrecise's own consumer or data-protection duties.

Gumroad's ability to keep recipient intake and delivery private from the buyer remains unproved. If the real account cannot present notice before collection, prevent buyer access, deliver to the recipient by default and preserve the required records, gift mode must remain disabled. Identify and assess any separate email, storage or delivery provider introduced by the final flow.

The public privacy notice contains a proposed, checkout-closed Studio section. It must be fact-checked against the final provider flow and owner-approved before Studio checkout opens.

### 9.5 Data protection complaints

Section 164A of the Data Protection Act 2018, inserted by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025, creates the controller-complaint duty. Publish a monitored electronic route that lets a person make a data protection complaint without putting birth details in the first message. Acknowledge the complaint within 30 days of receipt, make enquiries appropriate to its subject, keep the complainant informed about material progress and communicate the outcome without undue delay. Handle any data-rights request in the same message under the applicable rights timetable. Keep a minimal complaint log and preserve the person's right to complain to the ICO. The owner must assign the complaint handler and approve the operating procedure before launch.
