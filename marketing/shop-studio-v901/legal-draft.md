# AstroPrecise Studio — self-order legal implementation draft

Status: working review copy; not the current offer and not currently in force

Updated: 2026-08-24

This is operational drafting for a proposed UK consumer launch. It is not legal or tax advice and cannot replace advice based on the operator&rsquo;s confirmed circumstances. The public checkout stays closed until every fact and platform mechanic below is confirmed.

Insert and verify:

- legal operator name and whether AstroPrecise is a sole-trader business name or another entity;
- full public geographic business/service address;
- direct monitored electronic contact and complaint route;
- tax/VAT status, sale territories and tax-inclusive price display;

Do not infer any of these facts from a profile, draft or previous listing.

## 4. Contract formation and durable record

An order submission or deposit is an order request unless the owner and platform test prove and disclose another lawful acceptance event. Do not silently treat payment as acceptance.

Before work begins, send the buyer a durable confirmation containing the accepted product, self-order mode, total price/tax, delivery target, cancellation information and exact state of each optional choice. Attach the exact dated terms as a saveable file whose contents will not change when a webpage is edited. Record the attachment version and SHA-256 hash with the order. A link to mutable web terms alone is not the retained contract record.

The real signed-in test must prove that the customer can review and correct entries before submitting, and that the durable record reproduces the wording and state of the optional controls.

## 5. Delivery and quality description

Delivery is private and digital. Files go only to the verified buyer. Use generic filenames and include a customer SHA-256 manifest plus third-party credits.

The Natal A3 plate is an RGB home-print file, not a commercial CMYK press master. Whole Sky&rsquo;s Observatory still must visibly say `SCHEMATIC`; it is an authored compressed scene using computed positions, not a photograph, live feed or true-scale scientific rendering.

AstroPrecise will correct its own calculation, transcription, rendering and missing-file errors without charge. Buyer-requested new scope is separate and must not be used to relabel a statutory correction as a paid change.

## 6. Cancellation and optional early performance

A consumer may cancel the distance service contract without giving a reason during the 14 days after the day the contract is formed, unless the right has lawfully ended after full performance. Personalisation alone must not be described as making the service &ldquo;all sales final&rdquo;.

The buyer may cancel using a clear statement through the private order conversation and the published direct contact route, or the model cancellation form in the final terms.

### Separate optional early-start request

If the buyer does not expressly request early start, do not start the service during the 14-day period. The control must be separate, optional and initially unticked:

> I expressly request AstroPrecise to begin this personalised service during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that if I cancel after work has begun, I may have to pay an amount proportionate to the service supplied up to cancellation; and that if the service is fully performed within that period, I will lose my right to cancel once it is complete.

Record the exact notice version, SHA-256 wording hash, affirmative state and timestamp. No selection means no consent timestamp and no early work authority. If the buyer cancels after requested early work begins but before completion, charge only a lawful amount proportionate to work already performed. Do not charge where the required information or express request was not properly obtained.

### Separate optional early digital-supply choice

Supplying completed digital files during the cancellation period requires a second, separate, optional and initially unticked consent/acknowledgement. It must not be inferred from early start or general terms:

> I expressly consent to AstroPrecise supplying my commissioned digital files during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that I lose the cancellation right for that digital content when supply begins. This does not affect my rights if the service or files are faulty, misdescribed or not supplied with reasonable care and skill.

Record this wording version, hash, affirmative state and timestamp separately. If it is not selected, do not supply completed digital files during the period.

## 7. Refunds and statutory rights

For a cancellation refund, reimburse payments received less only any lawful proportionate amount for service expressly requested and supplied during the cancellation period. Reimburse without undue delay and no later than 14 days after being informed of cancellation, using the same means of payment unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise, with no reimbursement fee.

Nothing in the terms may limit rights to services performed with reasonable care and skill or digital content of satisfactory quality, fit for a disclosed purpose and as described. The platform process does not replace statutory correction, repeat-performance, price-reduction, refund or other remedies.

## 8. Personal-use licence

The buyer receives a personal, non-commercial licence to download, retain backup copies, display privately and self-print the supplied files. Resale, sublicensing, public-download redistribution, token minting and advertising use require separate written permission. Third-party material remains under its own credited terms. These licence terms do not restrict statutory rights.

## 9. Privacy implementation

The current shop preview collects no Studio order or birth data. Before opening checkout, publish a final notice grounded in the real tested flow.

For the self-order service, limit proposed processing to accepting, calculating, rendering, quality-checking, delivering, supporting, correcting and accounting for the buyer&rsquo;s commission. Necessary data may include buyer/order evidence; their own exact birth date/time/place; derived coordinates, timezone and UTC instant; product/contract choices; generated files; and quality evidence.

- Keep raw birth inputs inside the access-restricted order and fulfilment boundary.
- Never place them in public forms, URLs, analytics, marketing, filenames, public samples, source control or general logs.
- Never reuse them for marketing, profiling, training, public examples or unrelated research.
- Reject third-party birth data and follow the approved privacy/deletion procedure if it arrives unexpectedly.
- Delete raw inputs and local working files under the owner-approved, tested schedule; the proposal is within 30 days after final delivery or the final agreed correction, whichever is later.
- Retain only the minimum separate order/accounting evidence required by confirmed legal duties.
- Describe each real provider&rsquo;s role, fields, sub-processors, destination, transfer mechanism and retention; generic transfer wording is not proof.

Use contract necessity as the proposed basis for the buyer/self-order data actually necessary to take steps at their request and perform the contract, and legal obligation for required accounting records, subject to owner/adviser confirmation. Do not collect data merely because a platform exposes a field.

## 10. Problems, data rights and complaints

Publish a direct electronic service/cancellation route. Ask customers not to put birth details in a public form, URL or initial complaint message.

The data-protection complaint route must acknowledge receipt within 30 days, make enquiries appropriate to the issue, keep the complainant informed about material progress and communicate the outcome without undue delay. A rights request in the same message keeps its own legal timetable. The complainant retains the right to contact the Information Commissioner&rsquo;s Office.
