## Shared checkout/intake fields

Create these only in a private Commission flow that can prove the actor and preserve every required answer in a durable confirmation. Never ask for birth data in a public contact form, URL or buyer-only field. If Gumroad cannot support the two-person gift controls, enable `self` only and keep `gift` closed.

2. **Display name for the files** — required text, maximum 80 characters.
3. **Recorded birth date** — required text in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
4. **Exact recorded local clock time** — required text in 24-hour `HH:MM` format.
5. **Birth city or town** — required text.
6. **Birth country** — required text.
7. **Exact-time confirmation** — required acknowledgement from the chart subject:

   > I confirm that this is the exact local clock time recorded for the birth. I understand that AstroPrecise does not accept an unknown, approximate or rectified time for this launch service.

9. **Gift recipient controls** — required only for `gift`, completed by the recipient, not the buyer:

   - Recipient delivery email.
   - Adult/self-entry confirmation:

     > I am the adult chart subject and personally entered my own exact birth and contact details. I have read the versioned AstroPrecise gift privacy notice shown before these fields. I request their narrow private use to create, check, deliver and support my commissioned digital artwork and reading. I can object or ask AstroPrecise to stop; the buyer receives no birth details or files unless I separately authorise a buyer copy.

   - Optional buyer-copy authorisation, separate and initially unticked:

     > Optional: I authorise AstroPrecise to send the buyer a private copy of my personalised commissioned files as well as delivering them to me. I can withdraw this permission at any time; withdrawal cannot recall a copy already sent but prevents later buyer copies or replacements.

10. **Gift buyer attestation** — required only for `gift`, completed by the buyer and stored separately from the recipient declaration:

    > I confirm that I have not entered, uploaded, forwarded or dictated the recipient's birth details. The adult recipient is present and personally completing and confirming those fields.

11. **Optional birthday from-name and short message** — supplied by the buyer and visibly intended for the recipient; no hidden notes; message maximum 240 characters. Show: “Do not include health, beliefs, sexual-life information or details about anyone other than the recipient.”
12. **Optional early-start request** — separate, unticked checkbox completed by the buyer as the contracting consumer:

   > I expressly request AstroPrecise to begin this personalised service during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that if I cancel after work has begun, I may have to pay an amount proportionate to the service supplied up to cancellation; and that if the service is fully performed within that period, I will lose my right to cancel once it is complete.

13. **Optional early digital-supply consent** — separate, unticked and recorded only if the buyer asks to receive the completed digital files during the cancellation period:

   > I expressly consent to AstroPrecise supplying my commissioned digital files during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that I lose the cancellation right for that digital content when supply begins. This does not affect my rights if the service or files are faulty, misdescribed or not supplied with reasonable care and skill.

The early-start, early digital-supply and buyer-copy boxes are distinct choices for different purposes and actors. None may be bundled with general terms, preselected or described as required. If the platform cannot keep them separate and preserve wording, actor, state and timestamp in a durable record, do not launch the affected mode.

If the buyer requests early performance and then cancels before completion, any charge must be proportionate to the service actually supplied up to cancellation. The cancellation right ends on full performance during the 14-day period only where the required express request and acknowledgement were obtained.
