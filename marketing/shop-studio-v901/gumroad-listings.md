## Shared buyer input fields

Create these in the Commission product's Content/order-input area. Verify in a test order that every required answer appears in the private Sales drawer and durable buyer confirmation. Do not ask for birth data in a public contact form.

1. **Display name for the files** — required text, maximum 80 characters.
2. **Recorded birth date** — required text in `YYYY-MM-DD` format.
3. **Exact recorded local clock time** — required text in 24-hour `HH:MM` format.
4. **Birth city or town** — required text.
5. **Birth country** — required text.
6. **Exact-time confirmation** — required acknowledgement:

   > I confirm that this is the exact local clock time recorded for the birth. I understand that AstroPrecise does not accept an unknown, approximate or rectified time for this launch service.

7. **House system** — optional choice: Placidus (default) or Equal House.
8. **Optional early-start request** — separate, unticked checkbox:

   > I expressly request AstroPrecise to begin this personalised service during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that if I cancel after work has begun, I may have to pay for the service supplied up to cancellation; and if the service is fully performed within that period, I will lose my right to cancel once it is complete.

The early-start box must never be bundled with agreement to the general terms, preselected or described as required for purchase. If Gumroad cannot provide a separate optional unticked control and preserve the response in a durable record, do not launch until an equivalent compliant mechanism is implemented and reviewed.

If the buyer requests early performance and then cancels before completion, any charge must be proportionate to the service actually supplied up to cancellation. The cancellation right ends on full performance during the 14-day period only where the required express request and acknowledgement were obtained.
