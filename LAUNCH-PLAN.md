products without weakening privacy, payment or release controls.

- [ ] Create exactly three unpublished Commission drafts.

## Phase D — release authority

- [ ] Freeze the whole final candidate, not a narrow subset.
- [ ] Genuine native S12 and all declared specialist attestations.
- [ ] Owner signer installed and S1 imported last.
- [ ] Immutable `release/ap-v901-<sha12>` tag and protected environment checked.
- [ ] Workflow proves Pages deployment plus apex/www exact identity.

## Phase E — deliberate publication

- [ ] Owner confirms legacy Eclipse listing withdrawal; verify direct link, then
  archive while preserving past-buyer access.
- [ ] Logged-out review of three drafts, prices, files and legal links.
- [ ] Owner confirms publication of exactly those three listings.
- [ ] Add only verified checkout URLs through a governed commit/release.
- [ ] Make one controlled first sale; measure support, time, fees and deletion.

No paid ads, email platform, new model, physical product or subscription enters
scope until the first-sale proof is complete.
