On accuracy & privacy

Common questions

How we compute your chart, what stays on your device, and what you can verify yourself.

  • 01

    How is this different from sun-sign horoscopes?

    Sun-sign horoscopes assume you share a paragraph with everyone born in the same month. Astro Precise computes the full sky at your birthplace, to the minute — rising sign, Moon, houses, and aspects included. Two people born in July on different days, times, or cities receive different charts and different readings.

  • 02

    How accurate are the planetary positions?

    Astro Precise uses VSOP87, a published planetary theory. Positions are good to roughly an arcminute between 1800 and 2200 CE — tighter than any printed ephemeris you could buy.

  • 03

    Does my birth data leave my device?

    Your birth moment is computed on your device. The only outbound request is the place-name lookup — the place text you type goes to a geocoder to find its coordinates; your birth date and time never do. Nothing is stored in a database or attached to an account. Close the tab and it is gone.

  • 04

    Can I see the code?

    Yes. The engine is unminified JavaScript — open browser DevTools, find js/ephemeris.js, and read it. The math is inline. No black box.

  • 05

    Do I need an account for the free tools?

    No account is needed for the chart, the sky, or the daily horoscope. Paid readings require an email address so we can deliver the PDF — that is the only data we hold.
