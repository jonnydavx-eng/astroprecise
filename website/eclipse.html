LOOK SAFELY — THIS MATTERS

Even at ~91% coverage the remaining sliver of Sun is dangerously bright — the UK never reaches totality, so there is no moment it's safe to look directly. Use ISO 12312-2 certified eclipse glasses (check the standard is printed on them), a pinhole projector, or simply watch the light change around you. Sunglasses, phone cameras and exposed film do not protect your eyes. We take no cut on glasses — we just want you to keep your sight.
