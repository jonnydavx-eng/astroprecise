LOOK SAFELY — THIS MATTERS

Even at 96% coverage the remaining sliver of Sun is dangerously bright — Britain never reaches totality, so there is no moment it's safe to look directly and no moment to take the glasses off. Keep them on from first contact to last. Use ISO 12312-2 certified eclipse glasses (check the standard is printed on them), a pinhole projector, or simply watch the light change around you. Sunglasses, phone cameras and exposed film do not protect your eyes. We take no cut on glasses — we just want you to keep your sight.
