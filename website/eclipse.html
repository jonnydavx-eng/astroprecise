01 · Totality

Russia to Greenland to Spain

The path crosses northern Russia, Greenland, Iceland and Spain, then clips a small part of northeastern Portugal. Elsewhere across much of the Northern Hemisphere the eclipse is partial.

NASA path and overview
02 · UK and Ireland

90–96% coverage

London runs 18:17 · about 19:13 · 20:06 BST for first contact, maximum and end. Published tables round maximum by one minute. The Sun is low in the west; choose an open western horizon and check the time for your location.

Royal Observatory guide
03 · Watch live

Greenwich from 18:10 BST

The Royal Observatory’s UK stream runs 18:10–20:00 BST. NASA begins at 18:15 BST; ESA joins from Spain at 18:30 BST. Then stay outside: the Perseids peak overnight on 12–13 August, with the eclipse’s new Moon leaving a dark sky after twilight.

04 · Eye safety

Glasses stay on for every partial phase.

Use genuine ISO 12312-2 eclipse viewers or indirect projection. Phone cameras also need a solar filter over the lens; cameras, binoculars and telescopes need one secured over the front—not at the eyepiece.
