Your legal rights on digital purchases (UK)

Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you normally have 14 days to change your mind about a digital purchase. Because our readings are computed and unlocked instantly, at checkout you'll be asked to agree to immediate delivery and to acknowledge that the 14-day cancellation right ends once delivery begins — that's the standard UK rule for instant digital content, stated here in daylight rather than buried at the bottom.

Printed pieces (posters, numbered plates) are made to order for your exact chart, which UK law treats as personalised goods — but if one arrives damaged or wrong, that's on us: replacement or refund, your choice.

Our promise above the law. If the astronomy in anything we sold you is wrong — a genuine error in the computed numbers — you get a full refund, and we fix the engine in public. The whole site exists on one rule: computed, never fabricated. An error in the computation is the one failure we have no excuse for.
