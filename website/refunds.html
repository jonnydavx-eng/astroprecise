02 · THE 14-DAY RULE, HONESTLY EXPLAINED

UK and EU law gives you 14 days to change your mind about most online purchases. For digital content there is a standard exception: when you choose to unlock your reading immediately, you agree that delivery starts at once and that you waive the 14-day cancellation right for that item. That's the trade for instant delivery — you agree to it in the act of unlocking, never discovered afterwards.

If you buy and don't unlock, the 14 days stand — write to us and it's refunded, no questions.

03 · OUR OWN PROMISE — WIDER THAN THE LAW

If your reading is wrong about the sky — a position, a date, how close two planets really are — that's a defect, and you get a correction and a refund. If a quiet chart was sold a reading without the quiet verdict being shown first, that's our failure: full refund. And if within 7 days you simply feel the reading wasn't worth what you paid, tell us in one sentence and we'll refund it. We'd rather lose £2.99 than argue.

04 · WHAT NEVER CHANGES

Your statutory rights are untouched by anything above: content must be as described, fit for purpose, and of satisfactory quality (Consumer Rights Act 2015 in the UK, or your local equivalent). Nothing on this page reduces them; the promises here sit on top.

05 · HOW TO CLAIM

The receipt issued by the verified payment provider will carry the refund route and order reference. Refunds go back to the original payment method. No calls and no retention team. Questions first? Talk to a human.
