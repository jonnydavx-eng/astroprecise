Refunds & your rights.

No legal fog. Here is how buying works, what you agree to, and what you keep.

No product checkout is linked or opened on AstroPrecise. Voluntary support unlocks no product and no feature. This page also remains for past buyers of the archived 12 August Eclipse Edition. Existing licences, support and refund rights remain in place.

01 · Studio is a preview, not a current sale

Studio commissions are not yet available to buy. Prices, the payment and delivery sequence and the final refund terms will be published before any order can be placed. Nothing below is an active contract or an offer to sell.

02 · Proposed Studio 14-day cancellation route

For the proposed commissioned service, the buyer may cancel without giving a reason during the 14 days after the day the contract is formed, unless the right has lawfully ended after full performance. Personalisation does not create an “all sales final” rule. A clear statement through the private order conversation or published direct contact route is enough; a model form will also be supplied in the final terms.

03 · Starting early is optional

The proposed checkout must offer a separate, optional and initially unticked request to begin during the cancellation period. If it is not selected, work does not begin until the period ends. If it is selected and the buyer cancels after work starts but before completion, only a lawful amount proportionate to the service already supplied may be charged. The cancellation right ends during that period only after full performance and only where the required request and acknowledgement were properly recorded. Supplying completed digital files during that period needs its own separate optional consent and acknowledgement; it is not bundled with starting the service.

04 · How a cancellation refund is paid

For a proposed Studio service cancellation, AstroPrecise will reimburse the payments received, less only any lawful proportionate amount due for service expressly requested and supplied during the cancellation period. Reimbursement must be made without undue delay and no later than 14 days after AstroPrecise is told of the cancellation. It must use the same means of payment as the original transaction unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise, and no reimbursement fee may be imposed. Studio checkout will not open unless the payment route supports these rules.

05 · Corrections and statutory remedies

AstroPrecise will correct its calculation, transcription, rendering and missing-file errors without charge. That promise does not replace rights to reasonable care and skill, repeat performance, price reduction, refund or other remedies where they apply. Whole Sky is also proposed to include one reasonable layout adjustment requested within seven calendar days, using the same accepted birth details and agreed scope.

06 · Past Eclipse Edition buyers keep their rights

The archived Eclipse Edition was a digital good computed from the chart on the buyer’s device and unlocked with a licence key. Its birth data never left that device. Past purchases keep the licence, support and refund rights promised at purchase. This Studio draft does not narrow them, and AstroPrecise no longer links or opens that checkout.

If that reading was wrong about the computed sky, or a quiet chart was sold a reading without the quiet verdict being shown first, the preserved promise is correction and refund. The edition also carried the stated seven-day value promise. Contact us with the receipt reference.

07 · Voluntary support

Voluntary support is a gift, not a purchase: it unlocks no product and no feature. The support page shows whether support is one-time or monthly, and the amount, before you confirm. A monthly tip renews until cancelled and can be cancelled at any time through the account and billing controls of the support page you used. Support-payment and refund questions can also be sent to contact@astroprecise.app.

08 · How to ask for help or cancel

Questions, licence problems, cancellation statements and refund requests: contact@astroprecise.app, the private order conversation where one exists, or talk to the maker. Include the order reference, but do not put birth details in a public form, URL or social message. Any refund is returned to the original payer by the original payment method unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise, without a refund fee and within the applicable legal time limit.

Readings are computed astronomy with traditional meanings offered for reflection—not advice or prediction. This page is a plain-English summary, not legal advice.
