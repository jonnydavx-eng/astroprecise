3. Inputs and self orders

The buyer must be at least 18 and provide only their own known, exact, recorded birth date, local clock time, city and country, plus the display name requested for the files. Unknown, approximate and rectified times are not accepted at launch. Birth-time rectification is not included.

AstroPrecise does not accept another person’s birth or contact data in this first release. If an order or message contains third-party birth data, work must not start and the data must be handled under the published privacy process.

AstroPrecise will validate format, place and timezone before production. If details are incomplete, contradictory or affected by an unresolved daylight-saving ambiguity, work pauses while clarification is requested privately.

4. Production and delivery

The owner must define the exact contract-acceptance event before publication. An order request or deposit must not silently become acceptance under this draft. Before any work starts, the buyer must receive a durable confirmation email containing the accepted product, self-order mode, total price/tax, delivery target, cancellation information and every recorded optional choice. The exact dated terms must be attached as a saveable file whose contents cannot later change when a webpage is edited; record the attachment version and SHA-256 hash with the order. A link to mutable web terms alone is not the contract record.

The final checkout must match the signed-in platform test and state its electronic-contract steps: choose one of the three products; enter and review the required self-order fields; correct errors before submission; accept the governing terms; make each separate optional choice; submit the order request; and receive AstroPrecise’s acceptance or rejection. It must state whether and how the contract is filed and accessible, how input errors can be corrected, and that the contract language is English. The durable acceptance attachment is the customer’s retained contract record. These mechanics remain unresolved while checkout is closed.

Subject to final owner confirmation, the target is five working days beginning only after complete valid inputs, order acceptance and either the buyer's optional early-start request or expiry of the 14-day cancellation period. A working day is intended to mean Monday to Friday excluding bank holidays in England and Wales.

Delivery is digital through the private Gumroad Commission/order route. Files go only to the verified buyer. Customer files use generic names and include an integrity manifest. The A3 plate is an RGB home-print file, not a claimed commercial CMYK press master. Whole Sky's Observatory image is visibly labelled SCHEMATIC; it is an authored compressed view using computed positions, not a photograph, live feed or true-scale scientific image.

5. Cancellation and optional early start

A consumer may cancel the distance service contract without giving a reason during the 14 days after the day the contract is formed, unless the right has lawfully ended after full performance. Personalisation alone does not create an “all sales final” rule.

The buyer may send any clear cancellation statement through the private Gumroad order conversation and the published direct contact route, or use the model form below.

Optional early-start request

If the buyer does not ask for early performance, commissioned work will not begin until the 14-day period ends. Any early-start control must be separate, optional and initially unticked:

I expressly request AstroPrecise to begin this personalised service during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that if I cancel after work has begun, I may have to pay an amount proportionate to the service supplied up to cancellation; and that if the service is fully performed within that period, I will lose my right to cancel once it is complete.

Record the exact early-start notice version, SHA-256 wording hash, affirmative state and timestamp. An unselected control has no consent timestamp and cannot authorise work. If a buyer requested early performance and cancels after work begins but before completion, any charge must be proportionate to what was supplied up to cancellation. No early-period charge should be made when the required information/request was not properly obtained. The cancellation right ends on full performance during the period only where the required request and acknowledgement were recorded.

Optional early digital supply

If completed digital files would be supplied during the cancellation period, use a second, separate, optional and initially unticked consent and acknowledgement. It must not be inferred from early-start, general terms or buyer-copy authorisation:

I expressly consent to AstroPrecise supplying my commissioned digital files during my 14-day cancellation period. I understand that I lose the cancellation right for that digital content when supply begins. This does not affect my rights if the service or files are faulty, misdescribed or not supplied with reasonable care and skill.

Record this separate notice’s exact version, SHA-256 wording hash, affirmative state and timestamp. The durable contract confirmation must reproduce the wording and recorded choice. If it is not selected, do not supply completed digital files during the cancellation period.

Model cancellation form

To AstroPrecise: I give notice that I cancel my contract for the following commissioned service: Product: Gumroad order reference: Ordered on: Consumer name: Consumer address: Date: Signature (only if sent on paper):

Before launch, this form must also show the completed trader name, geographic address and direct electronic contact route.

6. Corrections and statutory rights

AstroPrecise will correct its calculation, transcription, rendering, missing-file and production errors without charge. Whole Sky is also intended to include one reasonable visual/layout adjustment requested within seven calendar days after delivery, using the same accepted birth inputs and agreed deliverables.

Nothing here limits rights to services performed with reasonable care and skill or digital content of satisfactory quality, fit for a purpose made known and as described. Gumroad's platform process does not replace statutory cancellation, correction, repeat-performance, price-reduction, refund or other rights where they apply.

For a cancellation refund, AstroPrecise must reimburse payments received, less only any lawful proportionate amount due for service expressly requested and supplied during the cancellation period. Reimbursement must be made without undue delay and no later than 14 days after AstroPrecise is informed of the cancellation, using the same means of payment as the original transaction unless the consumer expressly agrees otherwise, with no reimbursement fee. Checkout cannot open unless the tested payment route supports this.

Resale, sublicensing, public-download redistribution, token minting and advertising use require separate written permission. These licence terms do not restrict statutory rights.

8. Privacy and retention draft

Proposed processing is limited to accepting, calculating, rendering, quality-checking, delivering, supporting, correcting and accounting for the buyer’s own commission. Data includes buyer/order details; the buyer’s exact birth date, time and place; derived coordinates, timezone and UTC instant; product and contract choices; and generated files.

  • Buyer payment, order and self-order data are proposed to be processed because they are necessary for the contract. Required tax/accounting records are processed under legal obligation.
  • Birth inputs must remain in the private Gumroad order and access-restricted local fulfilment area.
  • They must not appear in public forms, URLs, analytics, marketing, filenames, public samples, source control or general logs.
  • They must not be reused for marketing, training, profiling, public examples or unrelated research.
  • Local raw birth inputs and working files are proposed for deletion within 30 days after final delivery or the final agreed correction, whichever is later.
  • Minimum order/accounting evidence is retained separately only for the legally required period.

Before checkout opens, the owner must approve the Studio privacy notice; confirm every provider’s role, data fields, sub-processors, destinations, transfer mechanism and retention; and prove buyer self-entry, private delivery, rights handling and deletion in an end-to-end fictional test. Third-party and gift ordering remain disabled until a separate lawful flow is designed and proved.

9. Problems and complaints

Use the private Gumroad order conversation for a calculation/delivery problem, cancellation or complaint so the order can be identified without posting birth details publicly. A direct electronic contact route will also be inserted before launch.

If AstroPrecise cannot accept a commission, it will be rejected and the deposit returned through Gumroad. Buyer-requested new scope must be agreed separately and must not be used to relabel a statutory correction as a paid change.

For a data protection complaint, the published route must satisfy the controller-complaint duty in section 164A of the Data Protection Act 2018, inserted by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025. It must let the complainant submit electronically without including birth details in the first message. AstroPrecise must acknowledge receipt within 30 days, make enquiries appropriate to the issue, keep the complainant informed about material progress and communicate the outcome without undue delay. A data-rights request in the same message must also be handled under its own legal timetable. The complainant keeps the right to go to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
