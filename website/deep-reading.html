About your reading

This reading uses AstroPrecise’s existing interpretation library and astronomical engine. Natal positions come from your chart; current planetary contacts are calculated when you open the page. Interpretations are symbolic traditions, not measured personality traits or predictions.

Without a birth time, rising and houses are omitted. The Moon is never assigned an exact degree. Sign changes identified in your chart remain open questions. All date-based degrees and contacts are approximate.

No birth details are sent to a reading service. The language templates load from this website; the reading is assembled in your browser.
