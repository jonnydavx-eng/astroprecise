Coverage note:
Uranus Cycle 33 color maps (2025a and 2025b) both have a large unobserved / night-side southern region filled with black in the official STScI product, plus a fringe at the coverage edge. That is Hubble's real map, not a convert error. Worlds did not paint the missing hemisphere.
Neptune 2025b is a full-latitude color mosaic.
