What it is:
Global mosaic built from three colour filter images taken by the Ralph/Multispectral
Visual Imaging Camera (MVIC) during the New Horizons flyby of 2015-07-14, draped on a
LORRI base. North is up. Sputnik Planitia — the left half of Pluto's "heart" — is at
the centre of the map; the dark red band across the lower third is Cthulhu Macula.

Processing: LANCZOS resize only to 2048x1024 (full webp + jpg), 1024x512 (_md webp),
512x256 (_sm webp + jpg). No painting, no colour grading, no channel reordering.

Coverage note:
Pluto's southern hemisphere was in winter darkness during the flyby, so roughly the
bottom third of the map is unobserved and is filled with black in the NASA product.
That black is real missing data and is reproduced as-is — the missing hemisphere is
not painted in. The far-side (non-encounter) longitudes are also genuinely lower
resolution than the encounter hemisphere; that softness is the data, not the resize.

A greyscale alternative exists if a panchromatic base is ever wanted:
  USGS Astrogeology, Pluto New Horizons LORRI-MVIC Global Mosaic 300m (Jul 2017)
  https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/pluto_new_horizons_lorri_mvic_global_mosaic_300m
  Public domain. Not shipped — the colour MVIC mosaic above carries Pluto's real hue.

No real public map of Pluto's ring system exists because Pluto has no rings, and no
clearly licensed public ring map exists for Uranus either, so no ring geometry is
built for either body.
