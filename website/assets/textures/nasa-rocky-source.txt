Rocky-body equirectangular maps — NASA / USGS hosted products
Local texture replacement only. No SKUs. No push. No sw.js bump.

Mercury
  File: Mercury_MESSENGER_ClrMosaic_global_665m_v3.tif (23054 x 11527, RGB)
  URL:  https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/Mercury_MESSENGER_ClrMosaic_global_665m_v3.tif
  Page: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mercury_messenger_mdis_global_color_mosaic_665m
  Product: MESSENGER MDIS Global Color Mosaic 665m, edition 3
  Credit: NASA / Johns Hopkins APL / Carnegie Institution of Washington /
          Arizona State University / USGS Astrogeology
  License: Public domain (USGS/NASA PDS)
  Bands: 1000 nm / 750 nm / 430 nm in R/G/B. Enhanced colour, not naked-eye.
  Coverage note: The official mosaic has small black rectangular data-gap tiles
  and a south-polar fringe. Those are in the USGS product and were not painted.

Moon
  File: lroc_color_16bit_srgb_4k.tif (4096 x 2048, 16-bit sRGB)
  URL:  https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_16bit_srgb_4k.tif
  Page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720  (CGI Moon Kit, NASA SVS ID 4720)
  Product: 2025 LROC WAC color map (643 / 566 / 415 nm), 0° longitude centre
  Credit: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio / LRO LROC (ASU) / LOLA
  License: Public domain (NASA)
  Coverage note: LROC WAC source covers 70°N–70°S. The official SVS product
  fills the poles with LOLA LDAM albedo and inpaints small dropouts. That fill
  is in the NASA file; nothing further was painted here.

Mars
  File: Mars_Viking_ClrMosaic_global_925m.tif (23059 x 11530, RGB)
  URL:  https://planetarymaps.usgs.gov/mosaic/Mars_Viking_ClrMosaic_global_925m.tif
  Page: https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_global_color_mosaic_925m
  Product: Viking Orbiter global color mosaic, 925 m/pixel
  Credit: NASA / JPL / USGS Astrogeology
  License: Public domain (USGS/NASA PDS)
  Honesty: Official product synthesizes green from red+violet (Viking green
  coverage is incomplete). ~98% red / ~95% violet coverage; gaps interpolated
  in the USGS mosaic, not by us.

Processing: LANCZOS resize only to 2048x1024 (full webp + jpg), 1024x512
(_md webp), 512x256 (_sm webp + jpg). No painting, no colour grading, no
channel reordering, no invented SKUs.
