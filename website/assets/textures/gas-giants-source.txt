Processing: LANCZOS resize only to 2048x1024 (full webp+jpg), 1024x512 (_md webp),
512x256 (_sm webp + jpg). No painting, no colour grading, no channel reordering.
Jupiter is a downsample from 3600x1800. Saturn is a modest upsample from 1800x900
so the rung sizes match the rest of the texture set.

Coverage note (measured 2026-08-14 on the STScI TIFFs):
Jupiter 2025a has 100 of 1800 latitude rows fully black — 36 at the north edge
and 64 at the south edge. That is Hubble's polar coverage gap in the official
product, not a convert error. Worlds did not paint the missing poles.

Saturn 2025a has 39 of 900 latitude rows fully black — 34 at the north edge and
5 at the south edge — plus a black equatorial strip in the official mosaic where
the ring plane occults / is masked. Both are in the STScI product and are
reproduced as-is. The equatorial black is the map's ring-plane mask, not a
shader artifact, and it is not painted out.

Rings (checked 2026-08-14, nothing swapped):
saturn_ring.* is left as the existing Solar System Scope strip. The OPAL Saturn
globalmap is a planet-body mosaic, not a ring-plane texture. No clearly licensed
NASA/ESA/USGS ring-plane map (a thin radial strip the engine can drape) was
found as a drop-in replacement, so the ring file was not invented or replaced.

Colour note:
These are OPAL filter composites (F395N / F502N / F631N), not natural-colour
photographs. Jupiter reads ochre/cream with a terracotta Great Red Spot; Saturn
reads pale pink-tan in the north and yellow-green in the south in the official
product. No channel reordering or grading was applied.

Uranus / Neptune / Pluto / Earth / Moon / Mercury / Venus were not touched.
