How a commission is made

You bring the minute.
We compose the sky.

The Studio accepts your own exact recorded birth time only at launch. If the time is approximate or unknown, do not order: houses and angles can change materially. Readings are for symbolic reflection and entertainment, not medical, legal or financial advice and not a promise of outcomes.

  1. 01
    You give us the recorded moment

    Date, exact local clock time, city and country. The timezone and coordinates are checked before calculation.

  2. 02
    The same engine as the free chart

    Placements are computed from your recorded sky, then composed as these plates — not merch themes, not Firefly, not a live photograph.

  3. 03
    The sitting is designed and checked

    Pages, dimensions, glyphs and filenames pass a quality gate before delivery.

  4. 04
    You receive the digital files

    Target production time is five working days after complete inputs and the applicable cancellation or early-start step.
