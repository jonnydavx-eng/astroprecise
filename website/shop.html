The commission

Recorded moment.
Computed sky.

The Studio accepts exact recorded birth times only at launch. If the time is approximate or unknown, do not order: houses and angles can change materially. For a gift, the adult recipient enters and confirms their own details. Readings are for symbolic reflection and entertainment, not medical, legal or financial advice and not a promise of outcomes.

  1. 01
    You provide your recorded moment

    Date, exact local clock time, city and country. The timezone and coordinates are checked before calculation.

  2. 01
    The recipient provides their recorded moment

    The adult recipient personally enters their date, exact local clock time, city and country, receives the privacy information and confirms the request.

  3. 02
    AstroPrecise computes the chart

    The same deterministic engine behind the free chart produces the placements and exact export geometry.

  4. 03
    The commission is composed and checked

    Pages, dimensions, glyphs, filenames and hashes pass a final quality gate before delivery.

  5. 04
    You receive private digital files

    Target production time is five working days after complete inputs and the applicable cancellation or early-start step.

  6. 04
    The recipient receives their private files

    Delivery goes to the recipient by default. The buyer receives a copy only when the recipient separately authorises it through an unticked choice.
