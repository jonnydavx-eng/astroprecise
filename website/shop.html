Before you look ahead

A few useful
details.

Digital, not physical
This planned pack contains seven downloadable files plus a print guide and personal-use licence. No printed item, frame or delivery by post is included. The proposed licence covers personal display and self-printing; printing by a third-party service is not included.
For your own recorded birth time
The proposed first release is for adults 18+ ordering for themselves, using an exact recorded birth time and Placidus houses. Approximate and unknown times can still be explored with the free chart.
One proposed price
£18 for the complete print pack is a draft price. The final checkout total and delivery terms are not yet available. Checkout remains closed.
Symbolic readings, calculated positions
The sky positions are computed. The reading offers astrological interpretation for reflection and enjoyment, without promises about future events.
