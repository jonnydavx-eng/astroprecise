Before you look ahead

A few useful
details.

Digital, not physical
These planned editions contain downloadable files. No printed item, frame or delivery by post is included.
For your own recorded birth time
The proposed first release is for adults 18+ ordering for themselves, using an exact recorded birth time. Approximate and unknown times can still be explored with the free chart.
Coming soon, not on sale yet
No prices are published and there is no way to order. Checkout remains closed, and there is no waiting list or sign-up. When that changes, it will be said plainly here.
Symbolic readings, calculated positions
Astrology on AstroPrecise is symbolic and offered for reflection and entertainment. The planetary positions are calculated carefully; the meanings are interpretation, not prediction or advice. Nothing here is medical, financial, legal or psychological advice.
