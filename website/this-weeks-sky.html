How honest is this?

Every event above is solved numerically from the VSOP87 planetary theory and the ELP2000 lunar theory, in your browser — the week is scanned, each event bracketed, then refined by bisection. Nothing is fetched from a server; nothing about you leaves this page.

Moon-phase instants are good to a few minutes. Ingresses and stations of the outer planets ride truncated series, so they carry a ≈ mark — right to within hours, occasionally a day for Pluto. The week is anchored to the UTC Monday, so every visitor sees the same list for the same civil week; only the clock times are local. Quiet weeks are shown as quiet — the list is never padded.
