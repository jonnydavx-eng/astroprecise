A note on the calendar

What these events mean.

Moon phases and eclipses describe light and shadow. Sign changes and stations describe positions in the tropical zodiac. Astrology reads them as symbols for reflection. They are not a forecast of what will happen in your life.

Event times come from our built-in ephemeris, checked against published almanac values. Approximate timings are labelled.
