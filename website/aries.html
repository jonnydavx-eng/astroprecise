About Aries

Aries is the first sign of the zodiac — the spark that begins the wheel. Ruled by Mars, the planet of drive and courage, Aries energy is direct, fast-moving, and unafraid of beginnings. Where others hesitate, Aries has already started. This is the sign of the pioneer: instinctive, competitive, and most alive at the moment of ignition.

Position on the ecliptic 0°–30° longitude

Aries is the 0°–30° band of the zodiac circle. The Sun crosses it around 21 Mar – 19 Apr each year. The exact day shifts by about a day from year to year, which is why the dates are approximate.

Aries in your chart

Most people meet Aries as a “star sign”. In a real birth chart it can appear in three very different places, and each one means something distinct.

Sun in Aries

Your core self: the “Aries side” people mean when they ask your sign. It shapes your basic character and what makes you feel most yourself.

Moon in Aries

Your inner, emotional world: how you feel, comfort yourself and respond in private. Two people with an Aries Moon share an emotional language, whatever their Sun sign.

Rising (Ascendant) in Aries

How you meet the world: your outward style and first impression. Your Rising is set by the exact time and place you were born, so it needs your birth time to know.

Aries strengths & challenges

Strengths

  • Courageous and decisive
  • Honest to the point of bluntness
  • Natural leadership instinct
  • Boundless initiating energy

Challenges

  • Impatience with slow processes
  • Acting before thinking
  • Difficulty finishing what was started
  • A temper that flares fast (and fades fast)

In life

Love

In love, Aries pursues. Attraction is immediate or it is nothing — this sign does not slowly warm to people. Aries partners are passionate, loyal once committed, and refreshingly free of games. The challenge is sustaining interest after the chase: an Aries needs a partner who remains a little bit unconquered.

Traditionally a good match with Leo, Sagittarius and Gemini. See two-chart compatibility: Sun signs alone are not the whole story.

Career, friendship, shadow, growth

Career

Aries thrives wherever speed and nerve are rewarded: entrepreneurship, emergency medicine, sales, athletics, the military. Routine is this sign's slow poison. The ideal Aries role has a scoreboard, a deadline, and room to act without committee approval.

Friendship

As a friend, Aries is the one who turns a quiet evening into an adventure and shows up first when there is trouble. This sign is fiercely protective and allergic to passive-aggression — say what you mean and an Aries will respect you for it. They lead the group, plan the trip, and start the dare, but the deepest loyalty they offer is simple: they will always have your back in a fight.

Shadow

The Aries shadow is the self that must win — even when there is nothing real to win. Unexamined, the drive curdles into aggression, the impatience into selfishness, and the courage into recklessness that leaves others to clean up. The growth edge is learning that not every disagreement is a battle, that patience is a form of strength, and that the bravest act is sometimes to wait.

Growth

The inner path for Aries is from impulse to intention — channelling raw fire into a purpose larger than the next conquest. This sign grows by finishing what it starts, by letting others move at their own speed, and by discovering that real courage includes the courage to be vulnerable. When Aries learns to lead for the group rather than the glory, the pioneer becomes the protector.

Frequently asked

What are the Aries zodiac dates?

Aries season runs March 21 – April 19. People born in this window have their Sun in Aries.

What element and ruling planet is Aries?

Aries is a Fire sign of the Cardinal modality, ruled by Mars. Its symbol is The Ram.

Which signs are most compatible with Aries?

Aries is traditionally most compatible with Leo, Sagittarius, Gemini. Full synastry depends on the complete birth charts of both people, not Sun signs alone.

What are the main Aries personality traits?

Aries is known for being courageous and decisive, honest to the point of bluntness, natural leadership instinct. Its growth edges include impatience with slow processes and acting before thinking. As a Cardinal Fire sign ruled by Mars, Aries carries the keyword “I am”.

Is Aries rare?

No zodiac sign is truly rare: every sign covers about a month, so each accounts for roughly one twelfth of birthdays. Small global birth-rate variations by season make some signs marginally more common than others, but Aries is no rarer than any other Sun sign. Your full birth chart, however, is genuinely one of a kind.

What is the Aries symbol and ruling body?

The symbol of Aries is The Ram, and it is ruled by Mars. Its polarity is masculine / yang, it is traditionally associated with the head and face, and its corresponding tarot card is The Emperor.
