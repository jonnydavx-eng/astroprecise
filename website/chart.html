How this chart is calculated +

Positions are calculated in your browser using the AstroPrecise astronomical engine. We use the tropical zodiac, Whole Sign houses and the true lunar node. In our checks against Swiss Ephemeris on 400 charts from 1900 to 2049, the planets, Sun, Moon and Chiron came within about 0.03°, the rising sign and Midheaven within about 0.02°, and the true node within about 0.1°. That is close enough for the signs and degrees shown here, but this page does not claim observatory-grade precision.

Your selected place supplies coordinates and a real time zone. Your browser applies historical clock rules. A repeated clock time asks you to choose; a time skipped by a clock change is rejected.

Without a birth time we use local noon (or the nearest minute that existed that day, if a clock change skipped noon) for approximate planetary positions, check sign changes across the local day, and withhold rising, houses and exact Moon degrees. Historical time-zone data and the precision of your birth record can affect the result.
