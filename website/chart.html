How this chart is calculated +

Positions are calculated in your browser using the AstroPrecise astronomical engine. We use the tropical zodiac, Whole Sign houses and the true lunar node. The built-in engine is approximate; this page does not claim observatory-grade precision.

Your selected place supplies coordinates and a real time zone. Your browser applies historical clock rules. A repeated clock time asks you to choose; a time skipped by a clock change is rejected.

Without a birth time we use local noon for approximate planetary positions, check sign changes across the local day, and withhold rising, houses and exact Moon degrees. Historical time-zone data and the precision of your birth record can affect the result.
