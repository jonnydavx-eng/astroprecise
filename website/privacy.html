Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

AstroPrecise is built privacy-first. When you use the free chart and Observatory tools, birth date, birth time and chart calculations stay in your browser. Place search sends the place text to Open-Meteo so your browser can resolve coordinates and timezone. If you choose “use my current location”, your browser sends that latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo to resolve its timezone. If you use the contact form, the contact details you deliberately submit leave the device; no chart is attached. If you choose voluntary Ko-fi support, Ko-fi and the creator’s connected PayPal or Stripe account handle the supporter and payment details described below. Gumroad holds the payment email and licence for the legacy 12 August Eclipse Edition, whose external listing remains reachable pending owner withdrawal; birth date, birth time and chart were never sent to Gumroad for that edition. The proposed Studio commissions are still a checkout-closed preview and collect no order or birth data on the shop page. If Studio opens, its private commission intake will necessarily handle the data described in the clearly marked Studio section below. This policy explains those limited data flows and the rights you have under the UK GDPR.

Who we are

The data controller is Jonathan Davenport, trading as AstroPrecise (a sole trader based in the United Kingdom). Gumroad is Merchant of Record for the legacy 12 August Eclipse Edition listing and its past orders. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of your rights, email contact@astroprecise.app.

The separate Studio trader disclosure, public geographic address, tax position and final direct service contact remain owner-confirmation gates. This privacy contact does not make the checkout-closed Studio preview an offer.

Studio preview and birthday-gift boundary

Studio checkout is closed. The shop page has no purchase form, no birth-data form and no email capture. Its “For me / As a birthday gift” choice stores only the word self or gift in this tab’s sessionStorage under ap-shop-intent-v1. It is not sent over the network and disappears when the tab session ends.

If the three Studio commissions open after legal and platform testing, self-order birth data will be entered by the buyer in the private order flow. In gift mode, the adult recipient must be present and personally enter their own exact birth details, see the just-in-time privacy information and confirm that they want the commission prepared. The buyer must never provide those details. Surprise gifts, gifts for under-18s and delayed voucher redemption are not supported at launch.

Gift files will be delivered to the recipient by default. The recipient pays nothing. A copy may go to the buyer only if the recipient makes a separate, specific and initially unticked authorisation after seeing what will be shared. That choice is optional, can be refused without affecting the order, and can be withdrawn at any time. Withdrawal before the first dispatch prevents that copy; later withdrawal prevents further buyer copies or replacements but cannot recall a file already sent.

What we collect

Birth details you type. Birth date, birth time, selected coordinates and the resulting chart are calculated in your browser and may be saved in this browser’s localStorage. Same-tab personal-sky handoffs and the couples comparison use sessionStorage, which is cleared when that tab session ends. When you search for a place, the place text is sent to Open-Meteo; if you choose current location, its exact latitude and longitude are sent instead. Your name, birth date and birth time are not included in either lookup. AstroPrecise does not create or accept chart links containing birth details. The default “Share this sky” image removes name, birth date, birth time and town. Downloadable export artwork includes the name, date, time and town shown on the chart, so review that artwork before sharing it. Gumroad retains payment email and licence records for that legacy edition; birth date, birth time and chart were never sent with an order.

Contact form. If you write to us through the contact form, your name, email address, and message are delivered to us via our form processor, Formspree. We use them only to reply. Do not put birth details, health information, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual-life information, or other special-category data in the free-text message.

Voluntary support. The Ko-fi support link leaves AstroPrecise. Ko-fi currently offers one-time and optional recurring monthly support. Ko-fi requires a supporter email and may provide us with the supporter name, email, message, transaction metadata and, for monthly support, recurring-payment status. Payment details are processed through the creator’s connected PayPal or Stripe account under those services’ terms. No AstroPrecise birth details or chart are attached to a support payment.

Proposed Studio commission records. If checkout opens, the private order flow will collect the minimum needed to fulfil the selected commission: buyer name/email, order and payment evidence; purchase mode and product; the chart subject’s display name, exact recorded birth date, local time, city/country and exact-time confirmation; derived coordinates, timezone and UTC instant; contract, optional early-start and optional early-digital-supply records; generated files and quality evidence. Gift mode additionally needs the adult recipient’s delivery email and recipient confirmation, a separate buyer attestation that they did not enter or direct the recipient’s data, plus an optional from-name/message. That free-text message is only for a short birthday dedication and must not contain birth details, health information, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual-life information, or other special-category data. The buyer attestation never replaces direct recipient evidence. Any buyer-copy authorisation is recorded separately with its exact notice version, SHA-256 wording hash, affirmative state and timestamp. AstroPrecise does not ask for health, belief, sexual-life or other special-category details.

Legacy order handoff. The dormant fulfilment bridge for previously configured products can receive a payment email, buyer name, order reference and product identifier from the payment provider. It removes those fields from the AstroPrecise address and browser-history entry before assets load, then passes them in the fragment of one specifically allowlisted Typeform URL. Typeform can read that fragment to match the service request; AstroPrecise never adds a saved chart name, birth date, birth time, coordinates or chart to this handoff. No current shop checkout uses this bridge.

Legacy listing and purchases. AstroPrecise no longer links or opens the 12 August Eclipse Edition checkout, but its external Gumroad listing remains reachable and marked in stock pending owner withdrawal. Gumroad retains the payment email, receipt and licence for past orders so a legitimate buyer can restore the edition or request support. Birth date, birth time and chart never left the device. A legacy external Gumroad listing may still be reachable; it is not a current AstroPrecise offer and must not be used pending owner unpublishing.

Email signups are paused. The current site presents no update-list form and sends no address to a mailing-list endpoint. If you subscribed before the pause, you can request access or deletion at contact@astroprecise.app.

Usage analytics — we run none. This site loads no analytics of any kind: no Google Analytics, no Umami, no Plausible, no tag manager, no tracking pixels, and no advertising or cross-site tracking scripts. We do not measure your visit, and we set no analytics or advertising cookies — which is why you have never seen a cookie banner here. The only records of your visit are the ordinary server and CDN logs described under “Security & hosting” below.

Lawful bases

  • Contract or steps at your request — processing voluntary support you choose to send, responding to a service request, supporting a legacy Gumroad order under the terms shown when it was purchased, and—if Studio opens—handling the buyer and self-order data necessary to accept and fulfil the commissioned service.
  • Legitimate interests, proposed for adult gift-recipient fulfilment — using the minimum recipient data to create and privately deliver the birthday commission the recipient has personally requested. This basis is not active while checkout is closed and remains a launch blocker until the controller completes and approves the recorded purpose, necessity and balancing tests. Recipient self-entry, immediate notice, no surprise/minor route, recipient-default delivery, short retention and an easy stopping/objection route are mandatory safeguards. The recipient may object at any time for reasons relating to their situation. AstroPrecise must pause future gift processing while it assesses the objection and stop unless it can document compelling legitimate grounds that override the recipient’s rights or the processing is needed for legal claims.
  • Consent for optional buyer-copy disclosure — if Studio gift mode opens, a buyer copy is sent only after the recipient makes a separate, specific, informed and unticked choice. The recipient may withdraw this consent as easily as it was given at any time, without affecting their own delivery. Withdrawal before the first dispatch prevents that copy; later withdrawal prevents further buyer copies or replacements. A file already sent cannot be technically recalled, so every dispatch requires a fresh validity check.
  • Legal obligation — minimum tax, accounting and consumer-rights records where law requires them. Raw birth working data is kept separate and is not retained merely because an accounting record is required.
  • Consent — any historical marketing-list record submitted before signups were paused. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — security and keeping the service reliable (for example the server and CDN logs our host keeps to serve and protect the site). These uses are minimal and proportionate, and you may object to them (see “Your rights”).

What stays on your device

localStorage on your device can hold saved charts, preferences and feature state needed to reopen the experience you chose. sessionStorage can temporarily hold a private sky handoff or couples pair within the same tab. We hold no copy of that browser storage. Clearing AstroPrecise site data in your browser removes it from that device.

Features that talk to other services

To provide certain features, your browser contacts third-party services directly. For the items below we do not receive your birth chart or reading content:

  • Place and timezone lookup — when you type a birth city, the place text is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to look up coordinates and timezone. If you press “use my current location”, its exact latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo’s weather endpoint for the timezone. Your name, birth date, and birth time are not included.
  • Sky-condition forecasts — our observing forecasts use the Open-Meteo weather API, which receives the location you ask about and standard request headers — never your birth data.
  • Live sky data — the Instrument page fetches public space-weather readings from NOAA SWPC and, for the “quantum draw”, a random number from the ANU Quantum RNG. These requests carry no personal information.
  • Fonts — none, on any page. Every typeface on this site is served from this site. Until 9 August 2026 eight secondary pages still fetched theirs from Google Fonts, which meant Google saw a request from your browser on those pages; they were cut over to the font files already bundled here, and no page loads type from anywhere else.

How long we keep things

  • Contact enquiries — 12 months, then deleted.
  • Historical marketing list — if you subscribed before signups were paused, your email address, tag and signup page are retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Security logs and rate limits — for the period needed to protect the service and investigate abuse; these records are not joined to saved chart data.
  • localStorage — until you clear your browser data; we hold no copy. sessionStorage lasts only for that tab session.
  • Proposed Studio raw birth inputs and local working files — delete within 30 days after final delivery or the final agreed correction, whichever is later, subject to owner approval and a tested deletion log that does not repeat the deleted data.
  • Proposed Studio order, payment and consent evidence — keep only what is needed for consumer, dispute, tax and accounting duties under the confirmed schedule; separate it from raw birth working data.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time (which does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal). Where processing relies on legitimate interests, you may object for reasons relating to your particular situation. AstroPrecise must stop unless it can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is needed for legal claims. Email contact@astroprecise.app and we will respond within one month. Chart and preference data on your own device is under your direct control — clear site data in your browser to erase it. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

If you are a proposed Studio gift recipient, you can refuse or stop the commission before work, object to legitimate-interests processing, correct your details and refuse or withdraw buyer-copy consent at any time. Contact AstroPrecise privately; do not put birth details in a public form or URL. Work must pause while an objection is assessed. A lawful objection or withdrawn optional copy choice will not be treated as a reason to pressure the recipient or route the request through the buyer.

Data protection complaints

This is AstroPrecise’s process for the controller-complaint duty in section 164A of the Data Protection Act 2018, inserted by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

You can complain directly about how AstroPrecise has used your personal data by emailing contact@astroprecise.app with the subject “Data protection complaint”. Give your name, a safe reply address, what you believe went wrong, the outcome you seek and—if relevant—an order reference. Do not include birth details or other sensitive information in the first message. If email is not accessible to you, use the contact page to ask for another reasonable way to submit the complaint.

AstroPrecise will acknowledge a data protection complaint within 30 days of receiving it, make enquiries appropriate to the issue, keep you informed about material progress and communicate the outcome without undue delay. A data-rights request made in the same message will also be handled under the applicable rights timetable. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at any time; using this direct route does not remove that right.
