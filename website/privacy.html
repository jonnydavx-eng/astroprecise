Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

Astro Precise is built privacy-first. Everything you can do here for free — casting a chart, reading the sky, the 3D model — is computed and stored on your own device, and we never see any of it. There is exactly one exception, and it only happens if you buy something we have to make by hand: then you type your birth details into a form and send them to us on purpose. It is described in full below. This policy explains, in plain English, the little we do collect, the lawful basis for each use, and the rights you have under the UK GDPR.

Who we are

The data controller is Jonathan Davenport, trading as Astro Precise (a sole trader based in the United Kingdom), of 2 Melton Close, Heywood, OL10 3DS, United Kingdom. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of your rights, email contact@astroprecise.app.

What we collect

Birth details you type. Your birth date, time, and place are used to compute charts and readings in your browser, and are saved in your browser’s localStorage so the site remembers your sky between visits. Nothing you type into the site is transmitted, and a purchase never carries them either — Gumroad tells us what you bought, not who you are astrologically. The one time your birth details do reach us is when you send them yourself: for a made-to-order item we cannot compute without them, the follow-up form after checkout asks for them directly. That form is hosted by Typeform (see the processor list below), we use what you enter only to make the thing you bought, and it is kept with the order record.

Contact form. If you write to us through the contact form, your name, email address, and message are delivered to us via our form processor, Formspree. We use them only to reply.

Purchases. Checkout is run by Gumroad, the seller of record. We receive your email address and an order ID so we can deliver your reading or print and support your order. Your payment card and billing details are collected by Gumroad on its own secure pages and are never seen or stored by us. We never receive your birth data with an order.

Email signups. If you join the newsletter or a “notify me” list (such as the eclipse-launch list), our own Cloudflare Worker stores, in Cloudflare’s encrypted key-value storage, the email address you submit, a topic tag recording which list you joined, the time, and the address of the page you signed up from (your browser’s Referer header — for example https://astroprecise.app/eclipse.html). Your IP address is stored separately as a counter, on its own, for one hour, purely to stop one machine flooding the list; it is never stored alongside your email address and it expires by itself. The same Worker sends one notification email to us through MailChannels, containing your email address, the tag and that page address.

Usage analytics — we run none. This site loads no analytics of any kind: no Google Analytics, no Umami, no Plausible, no tag manager, no tracking pixels, and no advertising or cross-site tracking scripts. We do not measure your visit, and we set no analytics or advertising cookies — which is why you have never seen a cookie banner here. The only records of your visit are the ordinary server and CDN logs described under “Security & hosting” below.

Lawful bases

  • Contract — delivering the reading, print, or key you bought, and handling your order enquiries.
  • Consent — marketing and notification emails. You give it when you subscribe, and you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — security and keeping the service reliable (for example the server and CDN logs our host keeps to serve and protect the site). These uses are minimal and proportionate, and you may object to them (see “Your rights”).

What stays on your device

localStorage on your device holds your birth details, the wallpapers and charts you generate, email-capture intents (so a half-finished signup is not lost), and unlock flags for readings you have bought. These items are strictly necessary for the features you use, so no consent banner is required for them. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser’s site data removes them completely.

Third parties & international transfers

Six companies are involved in running this site. This is all of them, and what each one gets:

  • Gumroad (US) — checkout and seller of record. Your email address, your order, and the card details you give it on its own pages.
  • Typeform (EU/US) — if you buy something made to order, the form you send us your birth details on is hosted by Typeform, so Typeform sees them. Reading or casting on the free site never touches it; that form is only reached after a purchase.
  • Formspree (US) — the contact form. Your name, email address and message.
  • GitHub (US) — hosting. The ordinary server log of your request.
  • Cloudflare (US/global) — content delivery and security in front of that host, plus our email-signup Worker and the encrypted storage behind it. If the 3D model’s graphics library fails to load from this site, the page falls back to a copy on Cloudflare’s public cdnjs service, which then sees a request from your browser. Nothing about you is attached to it.
  • MailChannels (US) — sends the one notification email to us when somebody joins a list, so it handles that email address and the page it was submitted from.

That is the whole list. No analytics, advertising or data-broker company receives anything from this site, because we do not use any. Some of these processors operate outside the UK; where they do, transfers are safeguarded by the UK–US data bridge, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or standard contractual clauses, as applicable.

Features that talk to other services

To provide certain features, your browser contacts third-party services directly. For the items below we do not receive your birth chart or reading content:

  • Place search — when you type a birth city, the place text (and, if you use the map, the coordinates) is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to look up coordinates and timezone. Your name, birth date, and birth time are not included.
  • Sky-condition forecasts — our observing forecasts use the Open-Meteo weather API, which receives the location you ask about and standard request headers — never your birth data.
  • Live sky data — the Instrument page fetches public space-weather readings from NOAA SWPC and, for the “quantum draw”, a random number from the ANU Quantum RNG. These requests carry no personal information.
  • Fonts — none, on any page. Every typeface on this site is served from this site. Until 9 August 2026 eight secondary pages still fetched theirs from Google Fonts, which meant Google saw a request from your browser on those pages; they were cut over to the font files already bundled here, and no page loads type from anywhere else.

How long we keep things

  • Contact enquiries — 12 months, then deleted.
  • Purchase and order records — 6 years, to meet UK tax and accounting requirements.
  • Marketing list — your email address, tag and the page you signed up from, until you unsubscribe.
  • Signup rate-limit counter — your IP address, on its own, for one hour. It expires by itself and nothing renews it.
  • Birth details sent for a made-to-order item — only as long as we need them to make and support that order, and deleted sooner if you ask.
  • Eclipse-launch list — deleted, or re-consented for ongoing news, after the 12 August 2026 eclipse.
  • localStorage — until you clear your browser data; we hold no copy.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time (which does not affect processing already carried out). Email contact@astroprecise.app and we will respond within one month. Chart and preference data on your own device is under your direct control — clear site data in your browser to erase it. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security & hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare; like any web host and content-delivery network, they see IP addresses and standard request headers in order to serve and protect the site. We take proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect the data we do hold, and we never sell personal data.

Children

Astro Precise is intended for adults. Purchases are restricted to over-18s, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “last updated” date above.
