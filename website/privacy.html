Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026

AstroPrecise is built privacy-first. Birth date, birth time and chart calculations stay in your browser. Place search sends the place text to Open-Meteo so your browser can resolve coordinates and timezone. If you choose “use my current location”, your browser sends that latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo to resolve its timezone. If you use the contact form, the contact details you deliberately submit leave the device; no chart is attached. If you choose voluntary Ko-fi support, Ko-fi and the creator’s connected PayPal or Stripe account handle the supporter and payment details described below. Gumroad holds the payment email and licence for the archived 12 August Eclipse Edition; birth date, birth time and chart were never sent to Gumroad. This policy explains those limited data flows and the rights you have under the UK GDPR.

Who we are

The data controller is Jonathan Davenport, trading as AstroPrecise (a sole trader based in the United Kingdom). Gumroad was Merchant of Record for the now-archived 12 August Eclipse Edition. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of your rights, email contact@astroprecise.app.

What we collect

Birth details you type. Birth date, birth time, selected coordinates and the resulting chart are calculated in your browser and may be saved in this browser’s localStorage. Same-tab personal-sky handoffs and the couples comparison use sessionStorage, which is cleared when that tab session ends. When you search for a place, the place text is sent to Open-Meteo; if you choose current location, its exact latitude and longitude are sent instead. Your name, birth date and birth time are not included in either lookup. AstroPrecise does not create or accept chart links containing birth details. The default “Share this sky” image removes name, birth date, birth time and town. Downloadable export artwork includes the name, date, time and town shown on the chart, so review that artwork before sharing it. Gumroad retains payment email and licence records for that archived edition; birth date, birth time and chart were never sent with an order.

Contact form. If you write to us through the contact form, your name, email address, and message are delivered to us via our form processor, Formspree. We use them only to reply.

Voluntary support. The Ko-fi support link leaves AstroPrecise. Ko-fi currently offers one-time and optional recurring monthly support. Ko-fi requires a supporter email and may provide us with the supporter name, email, message, transaction metadata and, for monthly support, recurring-payment status. Payment details are processed through the creator’s connected PayPal or Stripe account under those services’ terms. No AstroPrecise birth details or chart are attached to a support payment.

Legacy order handoff. The dormant fulfilment bridge for previously configured products can receive a payment email, buyer name, order reference and product identifier from the payment provider. It removes those fields from the AstroPrecise address and browser-history entry before assets load, then passes them in the fragment of one specifically allowlisted Typeform URL. Typeform can read that fragment to match the service request; AstroPrecise never adds a saved chart name, birth date, birth time, coordinates or chart to this handoff. No current shop checkout uses this bridge.

Archived purchases. AstroPrecise no longer links or opens the 12 August Eclipse Edition checkout. Gumroad retains the payment email, receipt and licence for past orders so a legitimate buyer can restore the edition or request support. Birth date, birth time and chart never left the device. A legacy external Gumroad listing may still be reachable; it is not a current AstroPrecise offer and must not be used pending owner unpublishing.

Email signups are paused. The current site presents no update-list form and sends no address to a mailing-list endpoint. If you subscribed before the pause, you can request access or deletion at contact@astroprecise.app.

Usage analytics — we run none. This site loads no analytics of any kind: no Google Analytics, no Umami, no Plausible, no tag manager, no tracking pixels, and no advertising or cross-site tracking scripts. We do not measure your visit, and we set no analytics or advertising cookies — which is why you have never seen a cookie banner here. The only records of your visit are the ordinary server and CDN logs described under “Security & hosting” below.

Lawful bases

  • Contract or steps at your request — processing voluntary support you choose to send, responding to a service request, or supporting an archived Gumroad order under the terms shown when it was purchased.
  • Consent — any historical marketing-list record submitted before signups were paused. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — security and keeping the service reliable (for example the server and CDN logs our host keeps to serve and protect the site). These uses are minimal and proportionate, and you may object to them (see “Your rights”).

What stays on your device

localStorage on your device can hold saved charts, preferences and feature state needed to reopen the experience you chose. sessionStorage can temporarily hold a private sky handoff or couples pair within the same tab. We hold no copy of that browser storage. Clearing AstroPrecise site data in your browser removes it from that device.

Third parties & international transfers

The current site relies on the following services:

  • Formspree (US) — the contact form. Your name, email address and message.
  • Ko-fi and the creator’s connected PayPal or Stripe account — optional one-time or recurring monthly support. Supporter name, email, message, transaction, payment and recurrence status as applicable; never birth data or a chart.
  • Gumroad — Merchant of Record for the archived 12 August Eclipse Edition. Past-buyer payment email, receipt and licence only; birth date, birth time and chart were never sent.
  • GitHub (US) — hosting. The ordinary server log of your request.
  • Cloudflare (US/global) — content delivery and security in front of that host.
  • Open-Meteo — place-name geocoding and, on observing pages, weather data for the location you request. Choosing current location sends its exact latitude and longitude for timezone lookup.
  • NOAA SWPC (US) — public space-weather readings; no AstroPrecise personal fields are sent.
  • ANU Quantum RNG (Australia) — a public random-number response for the optional quantum draw; no AstroPrecise personal fields are sent.

That is the whole current service list. No analytics, advertising or data-broker company receives anything from this site, because we do not use any. Some of these processors operate outside the UK; where they do, transfers are safeguarded by the UK–US data bridge, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or standard contractual clauses, as applicable.

Features that talk to other services

To provide certain features, your browser contacts third-party services directly. For the items below we do not receive your birth chart or reading content:

  • Place and timezone lookup — when you type a birth city, the place text is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to look up coordinates and timezone. If you press “use my current location”, its exact latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo’s weather endpoint for the timezone. Your name, birth date, and birth time are not included.
  • Sky-condition forecasts — our observing forecasts use the Open-Meteo weather API, which receives the location you ask about and standard request headers — never your birth data.
  • Live sky data — the Instrument page fetches public space-weather readings from NOAA SWPC and, for the “quantum draw”, a random number from the ANU Quantum RNG. These requests carry no personal information.
  • Fonts — none, on any page. Every typeface on this site is served from this site. Until 9 August 2026 eight secondary pages still fetched theirs from Google Fonts, which meant Google saw a request from your browser on those pages; they were cut over to the font files already bundled here, and no page loads type from anywhere else.

How long we keep things

  • Contact enquiries — 12 months, then deleted.
  • Historical marketing list — if you subscribed before signups were paused, your email address, tag and signup page are retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Security logs and rate limits — for the period needed to protect the service and investigate abuse; these records are not joined to saved chart data.
  • localStorage — until you clear your browser data; we hold no copy. sessionStorage lasts only for that tab session.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time (which does not affect processing already carried out). Email contact@astroprecise.app and we will respond within one month. Chart and preference data on your own device is under your direct control — clear site data in your browser to erase it. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Security & hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare; like any web host and content-delivery network, they see IP addresses and standard request headers in order to serve and protect the site. We take proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect the data we do hold, and we never sell personal data.

Children

AstroPrecise is intended for adults. Purchases are restricted to over-18s, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “last updated” date above.
