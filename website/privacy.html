Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

AstroPrecise is built privacy-first. When you use the free chart and Observatory tools, birth date, birth time and chart calculations stay in your browser. Place search sends the place text to Open-Meteo so your browser can resolve coordinates and timezone. If you choose “use my current location”, your browser sends that latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo to resolve its timezone. If you use the contact form, the contact details you deliberately submit leave the device; no chart is attached. If you choose voluntary Ko-fi support, Ko-fi and the payment provider it shows before confirmation handle the supporter and payment details described below. Gumroad holds the payment email and licence for the legacy 12 August Eclipse Edition, whose external listing remains reachable pending owner withdrawal; birth date, birth time and chart were never sent to Gumroad for that edition. The proposed Studio commissions are still a checkout-closed preview and collect no order or birth data. A final Studio notice describing the proved order flow will be published before any checkout opens. This policy explains those limited data flows and the rights you have under the UK GDPR.

Who we are

The current site identifies Jonathan Davenport as the data controller for AstroPrecise in the United Kingdom. Gumroad is Merchant of Record for the legacy 12 August Eclipse Edition listing and its past orders. For any privacy question, or to exercise any of your rights, email contact@astroprecise.app.

The owner must reconfirm the Studio operator’s trading status, public geographic address, tax position and final direct service contact; all remain owner-confirmation gates. This privacy contact does not make the checkout-closed Studio preview an offer.

Studio preview boundary

Studio checkout is closed. The shop page has no purchase form, checkout link, birth-data form or email capture, and it creates no Studio order record. The proposed first release is adult self-order only. Buying for another person and third-party birth data are not accepted.

Before Studio opens, this notice must be updated from a completed signed-in test to identify the actual order, payment, messaging, storage and delivery providers; the fields each receives; their roles and locations; the applicable lawful bases and retention; and the buyer’s rights. Until that is done, no Studio intake or fulfilment is authorised.

What we collect

Birth details you type. Birth date, birth time, selected coordinates and the resulting chart are calculated in your browser and may be saved in this browser’s localStorage. Same-tab personal-sky handoffs and the couples comparison use sessionStorage, which is cleared when that tab session ends. When you search for a place, the place text is sent to Open-Meteo; if you choose current location, its exact latitude and longitude are sent instead. Your name, birth date and birth time are not included in either lookup. AstroPrecise does not create or accept chart links containing birth details. The default “Share this sky” image removes name, birth date, birth time and town. Downloadable export artwork includes the name, date, time and town shown on the chart, so review that artwork before sharing it. Gumroad retains payment email and licence records for that legacy edition; birth date, birth time and chart were never sent with an order.

Contact form. If you write to us through the contact form, your name, email address, and message are delivered to us via our form processor, Formspree. We use them only to reply. Do not put birth details, health information, religious or philosophical beliefs, sexual-life information, or other special-category data in the free-text message.

Voluntary support. The Ko-fi support link leaves AstroPrecise. Ko-fi currently offers one-time and optional recurring monthly support. Ko-fi requires a supporter email and may provide us with the supporter name, email, message, transaction metadata and, for monthly support, recurring-payment status. The payment provider shown by Ko-fi processes the payment under its own terms; Ko-fi currently documents PayPal and Stripe as its supported providers, but the exact option shown can vary. AstroPrecise does not receive full card details. No AstroPrecise birth details or chart are attached to a support payment.

Studio preview. The current preview collects no Studio order, payment, name, email or birth data and creates no commission record. Do not send birth details through the contact form. The data fields for a future self-order flow will be disclosed here only after the real platform flow has been tested and approved.

Legacy order handoff. The former fulfilment bridge is retired. Its public page no longer reads order metadata, allows a form identifier or redirects to an intake provider. No current shop checkout uses this bridge.

Legacy listing and purchases. AstroPrecise no longer links or opens the 12 August Eclipse Edition checkout, but its external Gumroad listing remains reachable and marked in stock pending owner withdrawal. Gumroad retains the payment email, receipt and licence for past orders so a legitimate buyer can restore the edition or request support. Birth date, birth time and chart never left the device. A legacy external Gumroad listing may still be reachable; it is not a current AstroPrecise offer and must not be used pending owner unpublishing.

Email signups are paused. The current site presents no update-list form and sends no address to a mailing-list endpoint. If you subscribed before the pause, you can request access or deletion at contact@astroprecise.app.

Usage analytics — we run none. This site loads no analytics of any kind: no Google Analytics, no Umami, no Plausible, no tag manager, no tracking pixels, and no advertising or cross-site tracking scripts. We do not measure your visit, and we set no analytics or advertising cookies — which is why you have never seen a cookie banner here. The only records of your visit are the ordinary server and CDN logs described under “Security & hosting” below.

Lawful bases

  • Contract or steps at your request — processing voluntary support you choose to send, responding to a service request and supporting a legacy Gumroad order under the terms shown when it was purchased.
  • Legal obligation — minimum tax, accounting and consumer-rights records where law requires them. Raw birth working data is kept separate and is not retained merely because an accounting record is required.
  • Consent — any historical marketing-list record submitted before signups were paused. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — security and keeping the service reliable (for example the server and CDN logs our host keeps to serve and protect the site). These uses are minimal and proportionate, and you may object to them (see “Your rights”).

What stays on your device

localStorage on your device can hold saved charts, preferences and feature state needed to reopen the experience you chose. sessionStorage can temporarily hold a private sky handoff or couples pair within the same tab. We hold no copy of that browser storage. Clearing AstroPrecise site data in your browser removes it from that device.

Third parties & international transfers

The current site relies on the following services:

  • Formspree (US) — the contact form. Your name, email address and message.
  • Ko-fi and the payment provider shown before confirmation — optional one-time or recurring monthly support. Ko-fi currently documents PayPal and Stripe as its supported providers. Supporter name, email, message, transaction, payment and recurrence status as applicable; never birth data or a chart.
  • Gumroad — Merchant of Record for the externally reachable legacy 12 August Eclipse Edition listing and its past orders. Payment email, receipt and licence only; birth date, birth time and chart were never sent.

  • ANU Quantum RNG (Australia) — a public random-number response for the optional quantum draw; no AstroPrecise personal fields are sent.

That is the whole current service list. No analytics, advertising or data-broker company receives anything from this site, because we do not use any. Some providers operate outside the UK. The controller must keep provider-specific records of the relevant data location, role and transfer mechanism; a generic reference to a data bridge, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or standard contractual clauses is not proof that a particular transfer is covered. Any future Studio provider must be added here with accurate provider-specific information before it receives customer data.

Features that talk to other services

To provide certain features, your browser contacts third-party services directly. For the items below we do not receive your birth chart or reading content:

  • Place and timezone lookup — when you type a birth city, the place text is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service to look up coordinates and timezone. If you press “use my current location”, its exact latitude and longitude are sent to Open-Meteo’s weather endpoint for the timezone. Your name, birth date, and birth time are not included.
  • Sky-condition forecasts — our observing forecasts use the Open-Meteo weather API, which receives the location you ask about and standard request headers — never your birth data.
  • Live sky data — the Instrument page fetches public space-weather readings from NOAA SWPC and, for the “quantum draw”, a random number from the ANU Quantum RNG. These requests carry no personal information.
  • Fonts — none, on any page. Every typeface on this site is served from this site. Until 9 August 2026 eight secondary pages still fetched theirs from Google Fonts, which meant Google saw a request from your browser on those pages; they were cut over to the font files already bundled here, and no page loads type from anywhere else.

How long we keep things

  • Contact enquiries — 12 months, then deleted.
  • Historical marketing list — if you subscribed before signups were paused, your email address, tag and signup page are retained until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them.
  • Security logs and rate limits — for the period needed to protect the service and investigate abuse; these records are not joined to saved chart data.
  • localStorage — until you clear your browser data; we hold no copy. sessionStorage lasts only for that tab session.
  • Studio preview — no Studio customer record is created while checkout is closed.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and you may withdraw consent at any time (which does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal). Where processing relies on legitimate interests, you may object for reasons relating to your particular situation. AstroPrecise must stop unless it can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is needed for legal claims. Email contact@astroprecise.app and we will respond within one month. Chart and preference data on your own device is under your direct control — clear site data in your browser to erase it. If you are not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Data protection complaints

This is AstroPrecise’s process for the controller-complaint duty in section 164A of the Data Protection Act 2018, inserted by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

You can complain directly about how AstroPrecise has used your personal data by emailing contact@astroprecise.app with the subject “Data protection complaint”. Give your name, a safe reply address, what you believe went wrong, the outcome you seek and—if relevant—an order reference. Do not include birth details or other sensitive information in the first message. If email is not accessible to you, use the contact page to ask for another reasonable way to submit the complaint.

AstroPrecise will acknowledge a data protection complaint within 30 days of receiving it, make enquiries appropriate to the issue, keep you informed about material progress and communicate the outcome without undue delay. A data-rights request made in the same message will also be handled under the applicable rights timetable. You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at any time; using this direct route does not remove that right.

Security & hosting

The approved web host and content-delivery network serving a released version see IP addresses and standard request headers in order to serve and protect the site. Production verification must identify that provider and keep this notice aligned before publication. We take proportionate technical and organisational measures to protect the data we do hold, and we never sell personal data.

Children

AstroPrecise is intended for adults. Any future Studio purchase will be restricted to a buyer aged 18 or over ordering only for themselves, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
