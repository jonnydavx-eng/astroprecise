Numerology — and exactly what it is.

People love numbers that feel like they mean something, and numerology is one of the oldest ways of playing with that feeling. We keep it on the site because people ask for it — and we keep it honestly labelled, because that's the house rule.

What this is / what this isn't

The astronomy on this site is computed — real planetary positions you can check against any observatory. Numerology is different: it isn't astronomy, and there's nothing in the sky to compute it from. It's a number tradition — patterns drawn from names and dates, offered for reflection and entertainment, never as fact, advice, or prediction. We'd rather tell you that plainly than blur the line and hope you don't notice.
