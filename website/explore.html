The Living Sky

Loading the 3D model… if it doesn’t appear, your device may be running the simplified sky. The positions are still real — computed live from VSOP87.

Positions are live. Planet positions are computed on your device from VSOP87 — accurate to roughly a sixtieth of a degree (1800–2200 CE). The whole model runs in your browser.

Distances are schematic. The scale ladder below spaces things so you can see them, not to true distance. Each level says what it is:

  • Earth → System — positions live, distances schematic
  • Oort — an illustrative shell, not measured
  • Stars — directions schematic, not true 3D distances
  • Galaxy — a Gaia-style barred spiral, not a measured survey
  • Cosmos — decorative deep-field sprites
