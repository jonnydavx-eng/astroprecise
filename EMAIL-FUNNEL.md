# AstroPrecise email — deferred launch layer

Updated: 2026-08-24

AstroPrecise does not currently collect a shop waitlist and does not need to buy
an email platform to reach the first sale. Gumroad order/service messages are
transactional; they are not permission to add a buyer to marketing.

## When to add email

Revisit after the three-product checkout and one complete fulfilment are proven,
or after there is a real audience asking for launch updates. Until then, a signup
form adds privacy, consent, deliverability and support work without fixing the
current blockers.

If email is added:

- make consent optional, specific and unticked;
- state sender, content and frequency beside the form;
- store timestamp, wording version and source;
- provide one-click unsubscribe and honour it promptly;
- never send birth inputs, chart data or order details into the marketing list;
- keep transactional and marketing messages separate;
- update the privacy notice before collection starts.

MailerLite's public free tier observed on 2026-08-24 is 250 active subscribers
and 2,500 emails per month, replacing the repo's old 1,000/12,000 claim. Recheck
the official pricing before adoption; do not upgrade until real limits require
it: <https://www.mailerlite.com/pricing>.

No legacy Lemon Squeezy or PayPal funnel is part of v901.
