2. Place type in Photoshop / Illustrator. House tokens only: void #05080F, paper #E6ECF2, silver #8FA3B8, copper #B86B4A.
3. Caption: engine still. Type only. Not a live feed.
4. Export PNG and WebP. Delete the Stock / Firefly working file.
