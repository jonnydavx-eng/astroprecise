  minus ΔT 69.1 s = 17:45:57 UT = 18:45:57 BST, which rounds to 18:46 with a 3-
  second error; truncating to 18:45 is 57 seconds out. The site was already right.
  NASA's 17:45:51 UT plus its legacy ΔT 75.4 s gives the same TT anchor exactly,
  so the minute survives even the disagreement between the two ΔT values.

  including a session where I fixed the identical defect on the homepage coupon
  form and wrote a comment calling the technique load-bearing. I never checked

  interpretation copy only exists after a chart is cast, which is exactly where
  that vocabulary lives, and nobody looked there. Two separate "clean" scans were
