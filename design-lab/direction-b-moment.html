The eclipse itself

What actually happens

The Moon's shadow crosses Iceland and northern Spain, where the Sun goes out completely. Over Britain about 91% of it is covered — the deepest eclipse here since 1999. Greatest eclipse is 17:46 UT; the UK peak falls around 19:05 BST.

The one degree this page turns on

At greatest eclipse the Sun and Moon meet at 20°02′ Leo, computed from the Meeus series. That single degree is what your chart is measured against — nothing else about the event changes your answer.

Look safely

Britain never reaches totality, and at 91% the remaining sliver is still dangerously bright. Use ISO 12312-2 eclipse glasses or a pinhole projector. We sell no glasses and take no cut on them — we would just like you to keep your sight.
