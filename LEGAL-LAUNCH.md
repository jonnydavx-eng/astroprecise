# AstroPrecise legal launch boundary

Updated: 2026-08-24

This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice. The drafted Studio terms
are not a public offer until the owner supplies the missing facts and approves a
final review.

## Owner facts required

- exact legal operator and relationship to the AstroPrecise trading name;
- full geographic business/service address suitable for public display;
- monitored direct electronic contact route;
- countries from which orders will be accepted;
- tax/VAT position and Gumroad checkout treatment;
- approval of the five-working-day target, seven-day layout-adjustment scope and
  30-day working-file deletion rule;
- ICO fee exemption or payment/tier evidence;
- correct accounting-record retention schedule.

Do not replace these facts with guesses, a contact form or repository metadata.

## Public pages required before checkout

The final pages must agree on operator identity, address, contact, total-price and
tax presentation, Commission deposit/final-charge mechanics, service start and
delivery, optional early-performance request, 14-day cancellation handling,
proportionate charges after an early start, defects/corrections, refunds,
complaints, personal-use licence, privacy roles, retention and deletion.

Personalisation alone is not a basis for a blanket “no cancellations” statement.
Platform policy does not replace statutory consumer remedies.

## Data boundary

Birth date, local time, city/country, derived coordinates/timezone, order evidence
and generated files stay in private per-order storage. Never put real inputs in a
URL, analytics event, public form, sample, source repository, command line or
general accounting export. Do not use them for model training or marketing.

Raw inputs and working files use the owner-approved deletion schedule. Minimum
order/accounting evidence is retained separately for the applicable legal period.

## Platform boundaries

- Host paid commerce on Cloudflare Pages, not GitHub Pages.
- Use Gumroad Commission only after signed-in eligibility and full-flow testing.
- Treat Gumroad as a platform/merchant-of-record service where applicable, not as
  a substitute for AstroPrecise's own consumer and data-protection duties.
- Keep Ko-fi clearly labelled voluntary support.

## Review point

Once all owner facts are filled and the Gumroad test has fixed the exact platform
wording, a bounded UK consumer/e-commerce solicitor review is the only optional
professional spend worth considering. Reviewing placeholders earlier wastes the
fee.
