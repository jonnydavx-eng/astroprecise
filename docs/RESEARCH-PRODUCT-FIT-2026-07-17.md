# Product research fit — Astro Precise · 2026-07-17

Sources: market/app-dev trend pieces (2025–2026), Reddit r/astrology app threads, X product chatter.  
**Rule:** fit to *our* product (computed, private, honest) — not chase every trend.

## What the market is rewarding

| Trend | Evidence | Fit for Astro Precise |
|-------|----------|------------------------|
| Full-chart personalization (not sun-only) | Premium onboarding, “60s to value”; full chart vs 1/50 data points | **Strong** — free cast + engines already full chart |
| Daily / transit habit loop | Daily horoscopes, green/amber days, notifications | **Partial** — tonight + sky news + transit hit; no push notifications (by design / privacy) |
| Accuracy over Co-Star snark | Reddit: TimePassages, AstroMatrix, Time Nomad, Astro.com; Co-Star often dinged | **Strong differentiator** — Meeus on-device, quiet gate, verify plate |
| Visual / interactive charts | Animated natal, transit scrub, 3D | **Strong** — void orrery, natal sphere, sky news → fly model |
| Privacy | “computed on device” rare in big apps | **Core brand** — double down |
| AI chatbot / AI skincare astrology | Market noise | **Avoid as product core** — only if tied to real computed chart, never fake insight |
| Sun-sign gift lists / soft content | High X engagement | **Marketing only** — not product truth |
| Subscription fatigue | Reddit salt at Chani pricing | **One-time SKUs** already locked — good |

## Expert fit judgment (what to build next vs not)

### Build / polish (aligns)

1. **Real sky news as habit surface** — already shipped; keep feeding full-chart when saved chart exists (transit-to-natal).  
2. **Quiet gate honesty** — rare in market; keep visible on eclipse.  
3. **Verify / plate provenance** — “prove it’s real” answers Reddit accuracy anxiety.  
4. **Natal sphere + orrery door** — answers “animated chart” demand without faking NASA data.  
5. **Notify-me ladder** until Gumroad live — honest paywall placement.

### Do not build (trend trap)

- Sun-sign only daily spam  
- AI chat that invents aspects  
- Blockchain mint as launch hero (Lane 2 stays post-launch)  
- Subscription maze  
- Fake LIVE on stills
