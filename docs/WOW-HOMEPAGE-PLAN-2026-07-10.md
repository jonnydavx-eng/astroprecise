## Wow-Factor Patterns (web research — implementable in this stack)

Meta-finding across Awwwards/FWA 2025-26 winners, NASA Eyes, Stellarium, Apple pages, Bruno Simon, and Codrops build-logs: wow does NOT come from more 3D — it comes from ONE persistent hero model that the page keeps reframing (Shopify Editions, Sleep Well, Cartier, Codrops scroll-world all use a single fixed canvas across every section), scroll acting as camera director rather than page mover (normalized scrollY → damped camera waypoints, lerp 0.05-0.12/frame, quaternion slerp, snap-lock beats), an entrance ritual (loading-as-theatre handoff from poster to live render), constant micro-life (cursor parallax + idle drift so the model is never frozen), and real-data payoff at each stop (NASA Eyes' click-anything-zoom-to + Stellarium's time slider are their single most engaging features). OrbitLab already owns the hard parts — startScaleJourney/startCosmicFlight easing, setScaleLevel 0-6, getBodyReadout, captureFrame, GMST-true Earth, ±2000d scrub — the entire gap is BINDING page scroll/pointer to those APIs and staging one reveal. Apple ships 60-frame JPEG sequences because it has no live engine; a live-rendered scrub is strictly lighter and on-brand honest. Everything below is vanilla-JS implementable (rAF + scroll math + IntersectionObserver), no GSAP/Lenis needed, and respects warm-twilight eye comfort, the first-viewport cast form, and the 390px S24 tier.

### P1. Persistent fixed canvas — the SAME model reframes across every homepage section  ·  high/M

The signature 2025-26 award pattern (Shopify Editions, Sleep Well, Cartier, Codrops scroll-world): one position:fixed WebGL canvas behind transparent DOM sections; scrolling never swaps scenes, it re-aims the camera. Implement in website/js loader page: keep the one-Three-per-page canvas fixed full-viewport at z-index below content; define per-section camera 'beats' (hero+cast form = wide Earth framing; Sky path section = terminator close-up via the GMST True-Time Earth; daily section = geocentric sky framing; shop = slow beauty orbit). On each rAF, compute sectionProgress from getBoundingClientRect and lerp camera position/quaternion toward the active beat's waypoint. Reuse startScaleJourney's existing leg/easing machinery as the waypoint interpolator instead of writing a new tween system. This single change converts the 13k→5.5k px page from 'model in a box at the top' to 'the model IS the page'.

### P2. Scroll-scrub the 7-scale ladder in a pinned section  ·  high/M

Apple's core trick (pinned canvas + scroll-scrubbed frames) applied to the engine's best asset: a position:sticky section of ~500vh where normalized progress (0-1 from scrollY within the section) drives the Earth→Cosmos ladder — map progress*6 to a continuous parameter fed through startScaleJourney's leg interpolation (not discrete setScaleLevel jumps), with snap thresholds so each of the 7 rungs 'locks' like a page turn (Codrops snap-block pattern: snap only when |scrollVelocity| < threshold to avoid rubber-banding). Label each rung with its honest scale caption (schematic-labeled, on-brand). Scrubbing an entire cosmic zoom with your thumb is the site's defining wow moment and it's ~all existing engine code; per Builder.io/CSS-Tricks mechanics, the binding is 15 lines: progress = clamp((scrollY-sectionTop)/(sectionH-innerH)); camera lerp factor ≈ min(dt*6,1).

### P3. Curtain-raise entrance ritual: poster→live handoff choreographed as theatre  ·  high/S

Award sites treat load as reveal, not delay (VANA, Hubtown 'dark minimalist entrance'). The poster→lite→full loader already exists — add the missing 1.5s ceremony: show a captureFrame-generated poster instantly (cast form fully interactive on top, unchanged first-viewport), and when the full engine signals ready, cross-fade poster→canvas while the live camera performs one slow dolly-in (ease-out cubic, ~1.2-1.8s) as Earth's terminator/city lights rotate into view. The moment the static image 'wakes up' and starts truly spinning (GMST-locked, honest) is a one-time wow with near-zero added weight. Matches the already-spec'd Model Window/curtain-raise in MODEL-MASTERPIECE-SPEC-2026-07-10.md — this research strongly validates that spec; prioritize it.

### P4. Inertial damping + slerp on every scroll/pointer→camera binding (craft signal #1)  ·  high/S

Utsubo's cross-site audit: 'inertia and depth are what make it read as crafted, not templated' — winners never set camera directly from input. Enforce one rule in the 9.4k-line module: all external drivers (scroll, pointer, deck pills) write only to targetPosition/targetQuaternion; a single rAF damper does current += (target-current)*min(dt*k,1) with k≈6 (lerp 0.08-0.12/frame at 60fps) for position and THREE.Quaternion.slerp for orientation (prevents the classic multi-spin bug per three.js forum). Journey legs keep their own easing; the damper only wraps interactive input. Lower k on IS_PHONE tier if frame delta jitters. Small refactor, disproportionate feel upgrade on every interaction the site already has.

### P5. Promote the ±2000d time scrub to a hero instrument with orbit trails  ·  high/M

Stellarium's design critiques identify the time slider as 'the most engaging feature' — dragging it and watching the sky move in real time is the honesty-brand wow (real VSOP87 positions, no fake data). Surface the existing deck ±2000d scrub prominently in the hero/daily-sky beat (not buried in deck UI): while dragging, planets sweep along orbits with short additive-fade trail lines (cheap: ring of last-N positions per body, LineBasicMaterial, warm low-alpha per eye-comfort palette); on release, eased snap-back to 'now' with a 'Live — {date}' label so truth-state is always explicit. Ties directly into the Sky Wheel instrument spec. Also mirrors NASA Eyes, whose whole appeal is 'real data you can play with'.

### P6. Never-frozen model: cursor/gyro micro-parallax + idle drift  ·  medium/S

Hubtown/Lusion pattern: the hero responds to the mouse, so the model reads as alive before any scroll. Add ±2-3° camera offset from normalized pointer position, damped through the same lerp-0.05 damper (calm, not twitchy — respects eye comfort); on S24, a small deviceorientation tilt (amplitude-clamped, permission-gated, off if denied) plus an always-on idle orbit drift (~0.02 rad/s) so the model never sits static even untouched. Bruno Simon's case study: eased chase-camera + constant subtle motion is what makes his site 'feel alive'. ~40 lines on top of the existing camera rig; feeds targets, not camera, so it composes with journeys.

### P7. Model-moment deep links + one-tap share stills (NASA Eyes bookmark pattern)  ·  medium/S

NASA Eyes' event bookmarks with follow-camera are its shareability engine. Implement hash routes (#moment=saturn, #scale=4, #flight=cosmic) that on load skip straight to that camera state via setScaleLevel/startScaleJourney(leg)/body-focus, and pair every wow beat with a captureFrame button → downloadable/share-sheet still watermarked 'astroprecise.app'. This is already spec'd as model-moment deep links — research confirms it's the standard pattern; the extra insight is wiring captureFrame stills into the shop funnel ('wear your sky' = the share image IS the merch preview, per the existing shared print-design engine).

### P8. Live readout HUD with count-up on focus lock (honest wow)  ·  medium/S

When the camera locks a body (planet pill tap or scroll beat), animate in getBodyReadout data — distance, magnitude, constellation, rise/set — as a small labeled HUD card whose numbers count up over ~600ms (ease-out; tabular-nums to avoid jitter), warm-twilight ink at ~90%. NASA Eyes proves real-data readouts ARE the engagement, not decoration; this is the honesty-brand version of a wow moment and it differentiates from every fake-data astrology site. All data already comes live from the engine; work is one HUD component + a number-tween helper (~60 lines vanilla).

### P9. Cheap cinematic focus: FOV dolly-zoom + distance fog instead of postprocessing DoF  ·  medium/S

Real bokeh passes blow the mobile weight/GPU budget. Fake the depth-of-field 'focus snap' the way Primland does (atmospheric fog as depth cue): on body-focus, lerp camera.fov 50→~40 over 0.8s (updateProjectionMatrix per frame) for the dolly-zoom compression feel, while non-focused bodies get eased-down material opacity/luminance and scene fog deepens slightly (warm-dark fog color from the palette, never navy). Zero new passes, zero deps, works on the lite tier. Attach it inside the existing focus/journey code path so every focus event everywhere gets it for free.

### P10. Snap-beat choreography between narrative sections (velocity-gated)  ·  medium/M

Codrops scroll-world's key tuning lesson: free-scrub INSIDE a beat, snap-lock BETWEEN beats, or users park in half-transitioned mush. For the path/daily/shop beats use IntersectionObserver thresholds + a scroll-velocity check: when a section crosses 50% visible and velocity is below threshold, fly the camera to that beat's waypoint with a fixed 0.8-1.2s ease-in-out cubic + slerp (reusing the journey interpolator) rather than tracking raw scroll. Avoid CSS scroll-snap on the whole page (fights the pinned ladder section); apply per-beat in JS. Keeps every framing intentional — the 'every frame is about the one object' discipline Utsubo credits for award wins.

### P11. Ship Solar Masterclass as the site's second-act page (session-time multiplier)  ·  high/L

Game-studio marketing data: a playable/cinematic experience raises session time 5-10x vs bounce-in-30s pages, and 'scroll-driven narratives that unfold the world' is the indie-studio standard. The 7-chapter Masterclass exists in OrbitLab but isn't on the site — give it its own page (satisfies one-Three-per-page law; homepage links via a captureFrame poster card in the path section, so zero homepage weight), chapters advanced by snap-scroll or next/prev, each ending on a getBodyReadout fact card. This becomes the 'depth' proof that the homepage teases — Cartier's room-by-room structure is the direct model (self-contained chapters, one canvas, optional hidden cursor rewards per room).

### P12. Opt-in audio cues + perf floor (zone culling, offscreen pause) to protect the wow  ·  medium/S

Two small guards. (a) Audio: Bruno Simon calls sound 'the most powerful underused emotional lever' — add a default-MUTED toggle; when on, synthesize cues in WebAudio (no asset weight): soft warm chime on scale-rung lock, low pad during startCosmicFlight; never autoplay (brand = calm/honest). (b) Perf: the wow dies at 25fps — adopt the Codrops zone pattern (30→144fps): per-beat update functions early-return when their intensity is 0, aggressive frustum culling is valid because scroll camera paths are predetermined, and pause the rAF loop entirely when the canvas is offscreen (IntersectionObserver) or document.hidden — this also rescues Lighthouse mobile since the fixed canvas now lives on the whole page. Both slot into the existing capability-tier/IS_PHONE system.
