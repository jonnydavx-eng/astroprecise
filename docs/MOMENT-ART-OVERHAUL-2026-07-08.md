## Unified vision

**Moment** freezes any real date/time/place into a keepsake (zenith star + light-cone + optional chart layer + share card), then paths to paid PDF packs. It sits on the spine **Live sky → Chart → Moment keep → Daily → Reading → Shop** without a 7th primary nav item. Artwork uses Observatory 2026 void `#0C1016` + brass `#C2A05E` only.
