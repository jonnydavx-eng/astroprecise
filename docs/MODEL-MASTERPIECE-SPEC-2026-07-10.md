### Upgrades (ranked)

**S1. True-Time Earth: GMST-locked spin, J2000 pole, exact tidal-locked Moon, real tilts**  ·  impact: high · effort: M

Make rotation state a pure function of jd instead of incremental. (1) Earth: rotation.y = GMST(jd) + texture prime-meridian offset (GMST polynomial is ~3 lines; jd already flows through updatePositions at orrery-webgl.js:6017), replacing the += at lines 6360-6373 so setDate/scrub/Personal Sky all show the correct hemisphere under the terminator. (2) Pole: orient Earth's group quaternion so the spin axis points at the true J2000 pole direction expressed in the flattened scene frame (obliquity 23.44 deg toward ecliptic lon 270; scene mapping lon->(cos,-sin) per scenePos line 674, y compressed 0.35) — solstice/equinox terminator geometry becomes date-correct. (3) Moon: set rotation.y from its geocentric longitude + pi so the near side faces Earth exactly (true tidal lock) instead of the statistical 655.72h drift at line 6373. (4) Replace the generic 6-deg tilts (line 5510) with IAU values (Mercury 0.03, Venus 177 visual-equivalent, Mars 25.2, Jupiter 3.1, Neptune 28.3, Uranus 97.8) stored as new fields in orbitlab-bodies.js, and drive every body's spin phase from IAU W = W0 + Wdot*d constants (a 9-row table). Zero new draw calls, zero mobile cost; the 'live terminator' Masterclass caption (masterclass-chapters.js chapter 7) becomes literally true and the Personal Sky beat can honestly say 'this hemisphere faced the Sun at your birth'.

**S2. Saturn ring shadow cast onto the globe (reciprocal of the existing globe-on-ring shadow)**  ·  impact: high · effort: M

Extend the injected planet shader for Saturn only: add uniforms uRingTex (the already-loaded 2048x125 ring strip), uRingInner/uRingOuter (1.28/2.02 * size, from buildPlanets line 5674), uRingPlaneNormal (group up-axis in world), uSunDirW. In fragment: intersect fragment->sun ray with the ring plane, compute radial distance, sample ring density from the 1D strip, multiply diffuse by (1 - density * 0.85) with a smoothstep penumbra — the exact inverse of the technique already proven in saturnRingMaterial (line 5255-5272). Season-dependent shadow bands sweeping the globe are THE signature Cassini look and the single biggest photorealism jump for Saturn portraits/POD captures. Gate to mid+ tiers; low tier keeps the current shader (mobile fallback = no-op).
